### 水平衡（Water Balance） <a href="#zh-b0013" id="zh-b0013"></a>

水输入量 − 水输出量 = 0。实际水平衡为输入端水量与输出端水量之差。当输入端水量等于输出端水量时，即达到理想水平衡。

### 含水率（U） <a href="#zh-b0015" id="zh-b0015"></a>

湿质量中的水质量与干质量之比。含水率 = 湿质量中的水质量 / 干质量。

### 水足迹（Water Footprint） <a href="#zh-b0017" id="zh-b0017"></a>

评价产品和服务对水相关环境影响贡献的生命周期影响指标。

### 湿质量中的水质量（WWM） <a href="#zh-b0019" id="zh-b0019"></a>

湿质量减去干质量后所得到的水量。湿质量中的水质量 = 湿质量 − 干质量。

### 水清单（Water Inventory） <a href="#zh-b0021" id="zh-b0021"></a>

水足迹研究中的一个阶段，涉及汇编和量化与产品、过程和组织用水有关的输入和输出。

### 供水流（Water Supply Flows） <a href="#zh-b0023" id="zh-b0023"></a>

即供水交换；指技术圈内部的水交换（如自来水）。

### 用水（Water Use） <a href="#zh-b0025" id="zh-b0025"></a>

用于描述涉及水资源的人类活动的通用术语（包括农业、工业、能源生产、公共部门和家庭，以及捕鱼、休闲、运输和废物处置等河道内和原位使用），也指过程中使用的水总量。

### 直接用水（Water Use, direct） <a href="#zh-b0027" id="zh-b0027"></a>

指与给定过程直接相关的过程流。

### 间接用水（Water Use, indirect） <a href="#zh-b0029" id="zh-b0029"></a>

指与给定过程间接相关的过程流，例如该过程所用电力的生产相关用水。

### 取水（Water Withdrawal） <a href="#zh-b0031" id="zh-b0031"></a>

人为地从任何水体中永久或暂时取走水。

### 湿质量（WM） <a href="#zh-b0033" id="zh-b0033"></a>

样品或物体的湿物质质量。湿质量 = 干质量 + 湿质量中的水质量。

### Water Balance <a href="#en-b0013" id="en-b0013"></a>

Water input – water output = 0. Actual water balance is the difference between the amount of water on the input and the amount of water on the output. Ideal water balance occurs when the amount of water on the input if equal to the amount of water on the output.

### Water Content (U) <a href="#en-b0015" id="en-b0015"></a>

The ratio between water in wet mass and dry mass. Water content = water in wet mass /dry mass.

### Water Footprint <a href="#en-b0017" id="en-b0017"></a>

Life cycle impact indicator that assesses the contribution of products and services to water related impacts on the environment.

### Water in wet Mass (WWM) <a href="#en-b0019" id="en-b0019"></a>

The amount of water in the wet mass after subtracting the dry mass. Water in wet mass = wet mass – dry mass.

### Water Inventory <a href="#en-b0021" id="en-b0021"></a>

Phase of a water footprint study involving compilation and quantification of inputs and outputs related to water use for products, processes and organizations.

### Water Supply Flows <a href="#en-b0023" id="en-b0023"></a>

= water supply exchanges; are water exchanges within the technosphere (such as tap water).

### Water Use <a href="#en-b0025" id="en-b0025"></a>

Generic term used to describe human activity involving water resources (including agriculture, industry, energy production, public sector and households, in stream and in-situ uses such as fishing, recreation, transportation, and waste disposal) as well as the total amount of water used during the process.

### Water Use, direct <a href="#en-b0027" id="en-b0027"></a>

Refers to process flows directly related to a given process.

### Water Use, indirect <a href="#en-b0029" id="en-b0029"></a>

Refers to process flows indirectly related to a given process, for example water use related to electricity production used during the process.

### Water Withdrawal <a href="#en-b0031" id="en-b0031"></a>

Anthropogenic removal of water from any water body, either permanently or temporarily.

### Wet Mass (WM) <a href="#en-b0033" id="en-b0033"></a>

The wet matter of a sample or of an object. Wet mass = dry mass + water in wet mass.
