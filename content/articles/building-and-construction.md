## 建筑及建筑材料部门是高度能源密集的人类活动部门，也会加剧自然资源耗竭。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2024年11月19日

### <strong>部门</strong> <strong>概览</strong> <a href="#zh-b0004" id="zh-b0004"></a>

根据[联合国环境署](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/co2-emissions-buildings-and-construction-hit-new-high-leaving-sector)，建筑部门每年约占全球温室气体排放总量的 37%。

ecoinvent 数据库的建筑与施工部门包含 1,500 多个数据集，涵盖建筑矿物的开采、加工和运输，以及多数主要建筑材料的制造和寿命终结处理。覆盖地区包括巴西（BR）、哥伦比亚（CO）、秘鲁（PE）、厄瓜多尔（EC）、印度（IN）、南非（ZA）、突尼斯（TN）、瑞士（CH）、欧洲（RER）、加拿大（CA，包括魁北克省 CA-QC 和安大略省 CA-ON）、北美（RNA）和美国（US）。

ecoinvent 数据库包含建筑与施工部门中多种活动的数据集，涵盖建筑行业生产的不同阶段，从采石场或矿山开采建筑矿物（如砂、砾石、石灰石、黏土、石膏和天然石材），到熟料、水泥和混凝土生产。该部门也考虑在水泥中替代熟料的材料（如磨细粒化高炉矿渣、煅烧黏土），以及各种玻璃、保温材料（如聚苯乙烯、膨胀蛭石、岩棉）、砖、瓦、抹灰材料和砂浆的制造。

木材制造和加工数据集纳入 ecoinvent 数据库的[林业部门](forestry-and-wood.md)；建筑施工所用金属及金属工具生产的数据集纳入[金属部门](metals.md)。

### <strong>部门</strong> <strong>亮点</strong> <a href="#zh-b0009" id="zh-b0009"></a>

#### 资源开采 <a href="#zh-b0010" id="zh-b0010"></a>

本部门涵盖在采石场或矿山开采矿产资源的相关活动。这些资源随后用于生产建筑领域使用的材料和产品。数据集涵盖 23 种矿产资源（如砾石、砂和石灰石）以及 11 个地理区域：澳大利亚（AU）、巴西（BR）、加拿大（CA，包括魁北克省 CA-QC 和安大略省 CA-ON）、印度（IN）、南非（ZA）、欧洲（RER，另有瑞士 CH 和德国 DE 的专门数据）及全球（GLO）。

金属资源开采数据纳入金属部门。同时，[金属部门](metals.md)活动的许多副产品（如磨细粒化高炉矿渣、电弧炉粉尘）可用作建筑材料生产中的替代资源。

在材料生产中可从材料层面（如已分选的碎玻璃（glass cullet, sorted））或燃料层面（如废轮胎（used tyres）、废漆（waste paint）、废矿物油（waste mineral oil））替代原生资源的再生资源或废物，纳入[废物处理与回收部门](waste-management-and-recycling.md)。

#### 材料生产与加工 <a href="#zh-b0014" id="zh-b0014"></a>

本部门包含结构材料生产数据，覆盖巴西（BR）、哥伦比亚（CO）、秘鲁（PE）、厄瓜多尔（EC）、印度（IN）、南非（ZA）、突尼斯（TN）、欧洲（RER，另有瑞士 CH 和奥地利 AT 的专门数据）、北美（RNA）、加拿大（CA，另有魁北克省 CA-QC 和安大略省 CA-ON 的专门数据）、美国（US）和全球（GLO）；保温材料数据覆盖欧洲（RER，另有瑞士 CH 和德国 DE 的专门数据）、加拿大魁北克（CA-QC）和全球（GLO）；外立面材料数据覆盖欧洲（RER，另有瑞士 CH 的专门数据）和全球（GLO）；地面和屋面材料的地理范围与结构材料相似；路面材料覆盖全球（GLO）。本部门涵盖水泥和混凝土等同一类别内的广泛材料，其组成和性能会因在建筑中的用途而显著变化。部门提供符合国家及国际法规的具体水泥组成信息，例如 EN 197-1:2011、CSA A3001、ASTM C150、ASTM C595 和 ABNT NBR 16697。为满足不同市场对具体混凝土的需求，数据集包含从 15 至 50 MPa 的九个混凝土强度等级。数据集元数据可帮助缺乏专业知识的用户，根据暴露因素（如氯化物、湿度、冻融风化）和预期用途（如室内、室外、墙体、楼板、人行道、桥梁、桥面）信息选择最合适的参考材料。此外，对于强度等级未明确指定的情况，数据库还提供通用混凝土市场活动，例如“concrete, normal strength (25-37 MPa)”（普通强度混凝土）和“concrete, medium strength (40-50 MPa)”（中等强度混凝土）。

本部门还包括完整建筑数据库所必需的各种相关产品和市场活动。这些产品包括石膏板及玻璃棉、岩棉、石膏基和木基纤维板，以及各类泡沫等保温材料，主要覆盖瑞士（CH）和全球（GLO）。此外，本部门涵盖四个地理区域（瑞士 CH、欧洲不含瑞士、加拿大魁北克 CA-QC 和全球 GLO）的各类加工活动（切割、磨削、抛光、铣削），以及两个地理区域（瑞士 CH 和全球 GLO）的包装活动。

钢材和木材产品生产的数据分别属于[金属部门](metals.md)和[林业部门](forestry-and-wood.md)。材料寿命终结处理纳入[废物管理与回收部门](waste-management-and-recycling.md)。

#### 产品制造 <a href="#zh-b0018" id="zh-b0018"></a>

数据库包含建筑与施工部门所用产品的制造数据，包括结构产品（泥草混合料构筑（cob work）及各类混凝土砌块）、地面产品（各类地板和瓷砖）、墙体产品（各类砖和板材）、门窗、管道以及供暖、通风和空调（HVAC）部件。覆盖地理范围随可用数据集而异。多数覆盖欧洲（RER）和全球（GLO），另有专门表示瑞士（CH）和德国（DE）的活动。

#### 基础设施 <a href="#zh-b0020" id="zh-b0020"></a>

数据库包含资源开采（如采石场和矿山建设）、材料加工和产品制造所需基础设施的数据，覆盖可移动和不可移动的基础设施，包括输送带、工业和施工机械，以及专用于水泥制造的工厂。尤其是，本部门包含瑞士（CH）和全球（GLO）的水泥厂数据，近期更新（v3.11）又加入了加拿大（CA）的一体化水泥厂。其他基础设施和机械的覆盖范围取决于可用数据集，主要包括欧洲（RER）和全球（GLO），另有表示瑞士（CH）和德国（DE）的专门活动。

有关 ecoinvent 数据库中基础设施和机械数据覆盖范围的更多信息，请参阅[基础设施部门](infrastructures.md)。

## The building and construction materials sector is a sector of human activity that is highly energy intensive and contributes to the depletion of natural resources. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

November 19th, 2024

### <strong>Sector</strong> <strong>Overview</strong> <a href="#en-b0004" id="en-b0004"></a>

According to [UN environment](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/co2-emissions-buildings-and-construction-hit-new-high-leaving-sector), the construction sector contributes around 37% of the total global greenhouse gas emissions each year.

The building and construction sector in the ecoinvent database comprises of more than 1,500 datasets, covering the extraction, processing and transportation of construction minerals and the manufacturing and end-of-life treatment of most major construction materials. The geographies covered include Brazil (BR), Colombia (CO), Peru (PE), Ecuador (EC), India (IN), South Africa (ZA), Tunisia (TN), Switzerland (CH), Europe (RER), Canada (CA) including the provinces of Québec (CA-QC) and Ontario (CA-ON), North America (RNA) and the United States (US).

The ecoinvent database includes datasets on various activities within the building and construction sector. These include various stages of production within the construction industry, starting from the extraction of construction minerals (e.g. sand, gravel, limestone, clay, gypsum, natural stone), from quarries or mines, as well as the production of clinker, cement, and concrete. The sector also accounts for the use of alternative materials that can substitute clinker in cement (e.g. ground granulated blast furnace slag, calcined clay), along with the manufacturing of various types of glass, insulation materials (e.g. polystyrene, expanded vermiculite, stone wool), bricks, tiles, plasters, and mortars.

Datasets on wood manufacturing and processing are included in the [forestry sector](forestry-and-wood.md) of the ecoinvent database while datasets related to the production of metals and metal tools used in building and construction are included in the [metals sector.](metals.md)

### <strong>Sector</strong> <strong>Highlights</strong> <a href="#en-b0009" id="en-b0009"></a>

#### Resource Extraction <a href="#en-b0010" id="en-b0010"></a>

The sector covers activities related to the extraction of mineral resources in a quarry or a mine. These resources are then used in the production of materials and products that find their application in construction. Datasets cover 23 mineral resources (e.g., gravel, sand, limestone) and 11 geographies (Australia (AU), Brazil (BR), Canada (CA) including the provinces of Québec (CA-QC) and Ontario (CA-ON), India (IN), South Africa (ZA), Europe (RER) with dedicated data for Switzerland (CH) and Germany (DE), and the global geography (GLO)).

Data on the extraction of metal resources are accommodated in the Metals sector. At the same time, many by-products of activities in the [metals sector](metals.md) (e.g., ground granulated blast furnace slag, electric arc furnace dust) are used as alternative resources in the production of building and construction materials.

Recycled resources or waste, which may substitute primary resources in material production either at a material level (e.g., glass cullet, sorted) or at a fuel level (e.g., used tyres, waste paint, waste mineral oil), are included in the [waste treatment and recycling sector](waste-management-and-recycling.md).

#### Material Production and Processing <a href="#en-b0014" id="en-b0014"></a>

The sector contains data on the production of materials used for structure (Brazil (BR), Colombia (CO), Peru (PE), Ecuador (EC), India (IN), South Africa (ZA), Tunisia (TN), Europe (RER) with dedicated data for Switzerland (CH) and Austria (AT), North America (RNA), Canada (CA) with dedicated data for the provinces of Québec (CA-QC) and Ontario (CA-ON), the United States (US), and the global geography (GLO)), insulation (Europe (RER) with dedicated data for Switzerland (CH) and Germany (DE), Québec-Canada (CA-QC) and the global geography (GLO)), façade (Europe (RER) with dedicated data for Switzerland (CH) and the global geography (GLO)), floor (geographies similar to structure), roof (geographies similar to structure) and pavement (global geography (GLO)). The sector encompasses a broad spectrum of materials falling within the same category, such as cement and concrete. The composition and properties of these materials can vary significantly based on their roles within the building. The sector encompasses specific cement composition details in compliance with both national and international regulations (e.g., EN 197-1:2011, CSA A3001, ASTM C150, ASTM C595, and ABNT NBR 16697). To align the specific concrete with diverse market needs, the dataset incorporates nine resistance classes for concrete, ranging from 15 to 50 MPa. The metadata within the datasets are designed to assist users lacking specialized knowledge in selecting the most suitable reference material based on available information regarding exposure factors (e.g. chlorides, humidity, freeze-thaw weathering) and intended usage (e.g. indoor, outdoor, walls, floor panels, sidewalks, bridges, decks). Additionally, the database includes generic concrete market activities for cases where the resistance class is unspecified (e.g., concrete, normal strength (25-37 MPa) and concrete, medium strength (40-50 MPa)).

This sector also encompasses various related products and market activities essential for a comprehensive construction database. These products include gypsum plasterboards and a range of insulation materials, such as glass wool, stone wool, gypsum- and wood-based fibreboards, as well as various types of foams mostly under the Switzerland (CH) and Global (GLO) geographies. In addition, the sector covers different types of processing activities (cutting, grounding, polishing, milling) for four geographies (Switzerland (CH), Europe without Switzerland, Québec-Canada (CA-QC), and global (GLO)), and packing activities for two geographies (Switzerland (CH) and global (GLO)).

Data on the production of steel and timber products are respectively part of the [metals sector](metals.md) and the [forestry sector](forestry-and-wood.md). End-of-life treatments of the materials are included in the [waste management and recycling sector](waste-management-and-recycling.md).

#### Product Manufacturing <a href="#en-b0018" id="en-b0018"></a>

The database contains data on the manufacturing of products used in the building and construction sector. Such products include structural products (cob work and different types of concrete blocks), flooring (different types of floor and tiles), walls (different types of bricks and boards), windows, doors, plumbing and heating, ventilation, and air-conditioning elements (HVAC). The geographies covered vary depending on the available dataset. Most of the covered geographies are Europe (RER) and the global geography (GLO), however, dedicated activities are implemented for representing the Swiss (CH) and German (DE) geographies.

#### Infrastructure <a href="#en-b0020" id="en-b0020"></a>

The database contains data on the required infrastructure for resource extraction (e.g., quarry and mine construction), material processing, and product manufacturing, covering both mobile and immobile infrastructures. This includes conveyor belts, machinery used for industrial and construction purposes, as well as plants used specifically for cement manufacturing. Notably, the sector includes cement plants for Swiss (CH) and global (GLO) geographies, with a recent update (v3.11) including an integrated cement plant in Canada (CA). The coverage of other infrastructure and machinery varies depending on the available dataset and mostly includes Europe (RER) and global geography (GLO) with dedicated activities implemented for the Swiss (CH) and the German (DE) geographies.

For more information about the coverage of infrastructure and machinery data in the ecoinvent database, please consult the [infrastructure sector](infrastructures.md).
