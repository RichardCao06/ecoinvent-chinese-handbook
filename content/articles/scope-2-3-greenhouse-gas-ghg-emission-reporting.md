##

2023 年 9 月 29 日

《温室气体核算体系：[企业核算与报告标准（GHG Protocol Corporate Standard）](https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/standards/ghg-protocol-revised.pdf)》由世界资源研究所（WRI）和世界可持续发展工商理事会（WBCSD）制定，是计算企业温室气体排放的全球标准。

GHG Protocol 将温室气体（GHG）排放划分为三个不同范围。范围 1 温室气体排放也称直接排放，来自企业拥有或控制的排放源。例如，企业自有锅炉、熔炉或车辆燃烧燃料造成的排放。范围 2 和范围 3 排放也称间接排放。范围 2 排放是购买的能源（电力、蒸汽、热或冷）的生产所产生的温室气体排放。范围 3 排放是沿企业价值链发生、且不属于范围 1 和范围 2 的所有其他温室气体排放。

#### ecoinvent 中的范围 2 和范围 3 <a href="#zh-b0005" id="zh-b0005"></a>

[《GHG Protocol 企业价值链（范围 3）核算与报告标准》](https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/standards/Corporate-Value-Chain-Accounting-Reporing-Standard_041613_2.pdf)定义了范围 3 报告的若干类别，ecoinvent 数据库为这些类别提供丰富的二手数据。ecoinvent 数据库通过提供以下内容帮助报告编制者填补数据缺口：

- 区域或全球平均温室气体排放因子
- 平均运输距离和运输方式
- 区域产品组合和技术组合

随着[ecoinvent v3.8](ecoinvent-version-3.8.md)发布，电子表格“electricity emission factors – scope 2 – 3.xlsx”现已提供发电活动和电力市场分别对应范围 2 和范围 3 的排放因子（以 kg CO2 当量/kWh 电力表示）。这些排放因子代表电力最终消费者的情况，采用基于所在地的报告方法，即代表电网平均排放因子。此外，该新电子表格还提供按单种温室气体或温室气体组细分的排放量（以 kg/kWh 电力表示）和排放因子（以 kg CO2 当量/kWh 电力表示）。

可在<strong>电子表格“electricity emission factors – scope 2 – 3.xlsx”</strong>及其配套报告中获取这些信息；二者均位于[ecoQuery](https://ecoquery.ecoinvent.org/login)的“文件”（Files）部分。

##

Sept 29th, 2023

The Greenhouse Gas Protocol: [A Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard (GHG Protocol Corporate Standard)](https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/standards/ghg-protocol-revised.pdf) developed by the World Resources Institute (WRI) and the World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD) is the global standard for calculating corporate GHG emissions.

The GHG protocol subdivides greenhouse (GHG) emissions into three different scopes: Scope 1 GHG emissions, also called direct emissions, result from sources owned or controlled by a company. These are, for example, emissions caused by the combustion of fuels in company owned boilers, furnaces, or vehicles. Scope 2 and 3 emissions are also called indirect emissions. Scope 2 emissions represent GHG emissions from the generation of purchased energy (electricity, steam, heat, or cooling). Scope 3 emissions are all other GHG emissions that occur along a company’s value chain and are not part of scope 1 and 2.

#### Scope 2 and 3 in ecoinvent <a href="#en-b0005" id="en-b0005"></a>

[The GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Accounting and Reporting Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/sites/default/files/standards/Corporate-Value-Chain-Accounting-Reporing-Standard_041613_2.pdf) defines several categories for scope 3 reporting for which the ecoinvent database provides a rich pool of secondary data. The ecoinvent database helps reporters to fill data gaps by providing the following:

- regional or global average GHG emission factors
- average transport distances and modes
- regional product and technology mixes

With the release of [ecoinvent v3.8](ecoinvent-version-3.8.md), separated scope 2 and 3 emission factors (given as kg of CO2 equivalent/kWh of electricity) for electricity producing activities and electricity markets are now available in the spreadsheet “electricity emission factors – scope 2 – 3.xlsx”. The emission factors are representative for the end consumers of electricity and refer to the location-based reporting method, i.e., they represent grid average emission factors. On top of this, the new spreadsheet also provides disaggregated emissions (given as kg/kWh of electricity) and emission factors (given as kg of CO2 equivalent/kWh of electricity) for individual greenhouse gases or groups of greenhouse gases.

The <strong>spreadsheet “electricity emission factors – scope 2 – 3.xlsx”</strong> and an associate report can be found in the Files section of [ecoQuery](https://ecoquery.ecoinvent.org/login).
