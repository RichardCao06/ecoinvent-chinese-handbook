## 纺织品在全球广泛使用；其生产和消费所在的人类活动行业高度依赖土地、水和劳动力，并在多个层面影响环境。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2024 年 1 月 30 日

ecoinvent 数据库中该行业包含约 230 个数据集，涵盖天然原材料种植、加工和运输，以及相关过程产生的各种副产品的寿命终结阶段。

### 行业概览 <a href="#zh-b0005" id="zh-b0005"></a>

所覆盖的地理区域包括全球主要生产国印度（IN）和孟加拉国（BD）。数据库还包含全球平均数据。

ecoinvent 数据库包含纺织行业各种活动的数据集，包括用于服装纤维生产的作物种植（如棉花、有机棉花、黄麻、红麻、亚麻），其他纤维（如蚕丝）生产，人造产品（如聚酯、黏胶）制造，将植物加工为纤维，将纤维加工为纱线，通过不同工艺（如针织、针刺）生产纺织品，以及纱线和纺织品加工的辅助活动（如染色、防缩整理、漂白）。

许多用作纺织品生产数据集投入的数据集并不归入该行业。例如，纤维生产所用肥料的生产数据集包含在[化学品行业](chemicals-and-plastics.md)中。此外，所有灌溉相关数据集均包含在[供水行业](water-supply.md)中。

### <strong>行业</strong><strong>重点</strong> <a href="#zh-b0009" id="zh-b0009"></a>

#### <strong>纤维生产用植物</strong> <a href="#zh-b0010" id="zh-b0010"></a>

该行业包含用于纤维生产的各种一年生作物的数据。数据集遵循农业部门作物生产的建模原则。该行业包含六种天然纤维的数据，其中包括棉、黄麻、红麻、大麻和亚麻。地理覆盖包括主要生产国印度和孟加拉国。此外，还提供印度有机棉生产数据。

在排放和排放模型方面，所有作物生产相关数据集均采用一致方法建模。重金属排放计算基于 Agroscope 开发的[SALCA 方法](https://www.agroscope.admin.ch/agroscope/en/home/topics/environment-resources/life-cycle-assessment/salca-method.html)。该方法根据作物生产期间的投入（肥料、农药等），考虑镉、铜、锌、铅、镍、铬和汞向土壤与水的排放。此外，作物生产期间施肥所导致的向空气的 CO2 排放，按 IPCC 排放因子计算，并假设肥料中全部碳均以二氧化碳形式排放。

#### <strong>纤维、纱线和纺织品制造</strong> <a href="#zh-b0013" id="zh-b0013"></a>

包含纤维、纱线和纺织品生产信息。数据集包括原材料与水投入、直接排放、废物产生、基础设施投入，以及能源与材料消耗。此外，该行业还表示人造纱线和纺织品制造，包括聚酯、聚丙烯和黏胶纤维。同时涵盖纺纱、针刺、机织和针织等不同纱线与纺织品生产工艺。数据集覆盖纺织品产量最高的国家印度和孟加拉国。

#### <strong>辅助</strong><strong>活</strong><strong>动</strong> <a href="#zh-b0015" id="zh-b0015"></a>

辅助活动是将纤维、纱线或纺织品制成最终产品所需的过程，包括漂白、染色和防缩整理。辅助数据集对能源消耗、原材料投入、废物和废水产生以及排放进行建模，覆盖纺织品产量较大的国家印度和孟加拉国。它们可与纤维、纱线或纺织品数据集组合并调整，以对数据库未表示的产品建模。例如，“bleaching, textile”等辅助活动数据集可与任何纺织品数据集组合，创建表示相应漂白纺织品的数据集；或者不使用辅助活动，对未漂白版本进行建模。

#### <strong>全球</strong><strong>棉</strong><strong>花</strong><strong>生</strong><strong>产</strong> <a href="#zh-b0017" id="zh-b0017"></a>

该行业包含中国、美国和印度棉花生产的全球加权平均数据。这些数据由[Cotton Incorporated](https://www.cottoninc.com/)提供给 ecoinvent。该数据集遵循所有农业数据集的常规建模方法。

在排放和排放模型方面，该数据集采用一致方法建模。重金属排放计算基于 Agroscope 开发的[SALCA 方法](https://www.agroscope.admin.ch/agroscope/en/home/topics/environment-resources/life-cycle-assessment/salca-method.html)。该方法根据作物生产期间的投入（肥料、农药等），考虑镉、铜、锌、铅、镍、铬和汞向土壤与水的排放。此外，作物生产期间施肥所导致的向空气的 CO2 排放，按 IPCC 排放因子计算，并假设肥料中全部碳均以二氧化碳形式排放。

## Widely used across the globe, textiles are produced and consumed within a sector of human activity that is highly land, water, and labor intensive, and impacts the environment on multiple levels. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Jan 30th, 2024

In the ecoinvent database, the sector comprises of approximately 230 datasets, covering the cultivation of raw natural materials, their processing and transportation, and the end of life of the various by-products arising from the relevant processes.

### Sector Overview <a href="#en-b0005" id="en-b0005"></a>

Geographies covered include India (IN) and Bangladesh (BD) that are major producers on a global scale. The database also features global averages.

The ecoinvent database includes datasets on various activities within the Textiles sector. These include, cultivation of crops used for fibre production plants used for clothing (e.g., cotton, organic cotton, jute, kenaf, flax), production of other fibres (e.g., silk), manufacturing of man-made products (e.g., polyester, viscose), processing of the plants into fibres, processing of fibres into yarns, textile production with various processes (e.g. knitting, needle punching), as well as auxiliary activities of the processing of yarns and textiles (e.g. dying, sanforising, bleaching).

There are plenty of datasets that are used as inputs in textile production datasets but are not considered part of the sector. Such are datasets on fertiliser production (used in the fibre production), which are included in the [Chemicals sector](chemicals-and-plastics.md) Further, all datasets related to irrigation are included in the [Water Supply sector](water-supply.md).

### <strong>S</strong><strong>ector Highlights</strong> <a href="#en-b0009" id="en-b0009"></a>

#### <strong>Fibre Production Plants</strong> <a href="#en-b0010" id="en-b0010"></a>

The sector includes data on various annual crops used in fibre production. The datasets follow the modeling principles of crop production in the agriculture sector. The sector includes data on six natural fibres, including cotton, jute, kenaf, hemp and flax. Geographical coverage spans across the most producing countries, India and Bangladesh. Further, data on organic cotton production in India are available.

Regarding emissions and emissions’ models, all crop production-related datasets are modeled following a homogeneous approach. The calculation of heavy metal emissions is based on the [SALCA method](https://www.agroscope.admin.ch/agroscope/en/home/topics/environment-resources/life-cycle-assessment/salca-method.html)developed by Agroscope. The method considers cadmium, copper, zinc, lead, nickel, chromium and mercury emissions to soil and water, based on inputs during the crop production (fertilisers, pesticides etc). Further, calculation of air emissions of CO2 resulting from fertiliser application during crop production, is based on emission factors from IPCC, considering that all carbon in the fertiliser is emitted as carbon dioxide.

#### <strong>Manufacturing of Fibres, Yarns and Textiles</strong> <a href="#en-b0013" id="en-b0013"></a>

Information on the production of fibres, yarns and textiles is included. The datasets include the raw material and water inputs, direct emissions, waste production, infrastructure expenditures, energy and material consumption. Additionally, the manufacturing of man-made yarns and textiles, including polyester, polypropylene and viscose fibres, is represented in the sector. At the same time, different yarn and textile production processes are covered such as spinning, needle punching, weaving and knitting. The datasets cover countries of the highest textile production, India and Bangladesh.

#### <strong>Auxiliary </strong><strong>A</strong><strong>ctivities</strong> <a href="#en-b0015" id="en-b0015"></a>

Auxiliary activities refer to processes necessary for the preparation of fibres, yarns or textiles into finished products. They include bleaching, dying and sanforising. The auxiliary datasets model energy consumption, inputs of raw materials, waste and wastewater production as well as emissions, while they cover the countries with the larger volumes of textile production, India and Bangladesh. They can be used in combination with fibre, yarn or textile datasets, adjusted to model a product that is not represented in the database. For example, auxiliary activities, such as the dataset “bleaching, textile”, can be used along with any textile dataset to create one representing the respective bleached textile; alternatively an unbleached version can be modeled without using the auxiliary activity.

#### <strong>Global </strong><strong>C</strong><strong>otton </strong><strong>P</strong><strong>roduction</strong> <a href="#en-b0017" id="en-b0017"></a>

The sector includes a global weighted average production of cotton in China, United States and India. The data have been provided to ecoinvent by [Cotton Incorporated](https://www.cottoninc.com/). The dataset follows the regular modeling approach of all agricultural datasets.

Regarding emissions and emissions’ models, the dataset is modeled following a homogeneous approach. The calculation of heavy metal emissions is based on the [SALCA method](https://www.agroscope.admin.ch/agroscope/en/home/topics/environment-resources/life-cycle-assessment/salca-method.html) developed by Agroscope. The method considers cadmium, copper, zinc, lead, nickel, chromium and mercury emissions to soil and water, based on inputs during the crop production (fertilisers, pesticides etc). Further, calculation of air emissions of CO2 resulting from fertiliser application during crop production, is based on emission factors from IPCC, considering that all carbon in the fertiliser is emitted as carbon dioxide
