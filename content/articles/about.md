### <strong>ecoinvent 数据库的数据来自哪里？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

数据可以通过多种途径进入 ecoinvent 数据库；无论哪种情况，ecoinvent 都会获得非独占的数据发布权，但数据创建者仍是数据所有者。

我们的多数清单数据集是在针对特定经济部门及国家或地区的数据收集项目中生成的。为开展这些项目，我们通常与具有当地、行业及方法学专业知识的全球外部合作伙伴合作，例如研究机构、高校、咨询公司或行业协会。我们的团队也为主动联系我们、希望在 ecoinvent 发布数据的研究人员和企业提供支持。此外，部分项目由我们规模不大的内部 LCA 专家团队开展。

因此，我们的数据来源多种多样。在条件允许时，外部合作伙伴会提供一手数据，例如实地走访或访谈所得的数据。二手数据优先采用公开统计资料、经过同行评审的科学文献和数据库，并辅以企业报告、排放登记册等其他公开的行业数据。

无论采用何种数据收集方式，所有数据集在发布到数据库之前，都要经过内部专家和独立外部专家的全面审查，以确保满足 ecoinvent 的质量和透明度要求。

关于 ecoinvent 数据库内容的更深入信息，请参阅[数据库介绍](introduction-to-the-database.md)。

新用户请参阅支持页面，了解如何[开始使用](https://support.ecoinvent.org/getting-started) ecoinvent。

### <strong>ISO 27001 认证</strong> <a href="#zh-b0018" id="zh-b0018"></a>

我们的信息安全管理体系（ISMS）已由 Prescient Security 正式认证符合 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 标准。该体系涵盖 ecoQuery、UMS 与账户管理服务，以及 HubSpot 上的知识库和支持渠道。

认证范围还涵盖软件产品开发、IT、人力资源等部门相关的人员、流程、技术、法律、监管和合同要求。

ISO 27001 标准被全球公认为信息安全最佳实践。它有助于更好地保护数据，并体现采用最新安全措施的承诺。获得 ISO 27001 认证意味着您可以信赖 ecoinvent 以合规、负责和尊重的态度管理您的数据。

### <strong>Where Does the Data in the ecoinvent Database Come From?</strong> <a href="#en-b0011" id="en-b0011"></a>

There are multiple paths through which data can find its way into the ecoinvent database; in all cases, ecoinvent obtains the right to publish the data non-exclusively, but the data creator remains the data owner.

Most of our inventory datasets are generated in data collection projects dedicated to specific economic sectors and countries or regions. To realize these projects, we commonly collaborate with external global partners with local, sectoral, and methodological expertise, such as research institutes, academia, consultancies, or industry associations. Our team also supports individual researchers and companies who directly approach us with data they want to publish in ecoinvent. Finally, some projects are carried out internally by our small team of LCA experts.

Accordingly, the sources our data relies on are diverse. Where possible, our external partners will provide primary data, for example, from field visits or interviews. Secondary data are preferably sourced from publicly available statistics, peer-reviewed scientific literature, and databases complemented with other public (industry) data such as company reports, emission registers, etc.

Regardless of the type of data collection, all datasets undergo a thorough review from internal and independent external experts to ensure they fulfill ecoinvent quality and transparency requirements before they are published in the database.

More in-depth information about the contents of the ecoinvent database can be found in our [Introduction to the Database](introduction-to-the-database.md).

New users should consult our support pages for [Getting Started](https://support.ecoinvent.org/getting-started) with ecoinvent.

### <strong>ISO 27001 Certification</strong> <a href="#en-b0018" id="en-b0018"></a>

Our Information Security Management System (ISMS), including ecoQuery, our UMS & Account management service, and our Knowledge Base & Support channel on HubSpot, is officially ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified by Prescient Security.

The scope also includes the people, process, technology, legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements related to the departments such as Software product development, IT, and HR.

The ISO 27001 standard is recognized worldwide as best practice in information security. It ensures better protection of data and a commitment to enabling the latest security measures. With an ISO 27001 certification, you can trust ecoinvent to manage your data with compliance, responsibility, and respect.
