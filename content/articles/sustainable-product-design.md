##

2023年9月29日

在产品上市前的设计阶段采用安全、可持续的设计方法，可以提高产品安全性、降低污染对人类健康和环境健康的风险，并支持全球向循环经济和低碳经济转型。

ecoinvent 数据库包含与大多数产品系统密切相关的核心经济产品／活动的信息，使设计者能够集中精力处理与其系统最相关的产品／活动（前景数据）。

ecoinvent 数据是多种可持续产品设计软件工具的基础。以下是一些示例：

<strong>Dassault Systèmes</strong> | [CATIA](https://www.3ds.com/products-services/catia/)

<strong>Trace One（原 Selerant）</strong> | [EcodEX](https://www.traceone.com/sustainability-software)

<strong>Ecochain</strong> | [Ecochain](https://ecochain.com/)

<strong>Trayak</strong> | [EcoImpact](https://trayak.com/ecoimpact-new-features)

##

Sept 29th, 2023

Applying a safe and sustainable approach to design in products’ pre-market design phase can improve product safety, reduce the risks to human and environmental health from pollution, and support the world’s transition to a circular and low-carbon economy.

The ecoinvent database includes information on core economic products/activities of high relevance to most product systems, allowing designers to focus their efforts on the products/activities most relevant for their systems (foreground data).

ecoinvent data underlies several software tools employed in Sustainable Product Design. Explore some examples:

<strong>Dassault Systèmes</strong> | [CATIA](https://www.3ds.com/products-services/catia/)

<strong>Trace One (formerly Selerant) </strong>| [EcodEX](https://www.traceone.com/sustainability-software)

<strong>Ecochain</strong> | [Ecochain](https://ecochain.com/)
