## 林业与木材行业是专注于木材采伐及加工、以满足各种应用的人类活动领域。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2024 年 4 月 10 日

[全球 60% 的森林](https://www.un.org/esa/forests/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/UNFF13_BkgdStudy_ForestsSCP.pdf)主要或部分用于生产木材和非木材产品。在 ecoinvent 数据库中，该行业包含约 800 个数据集，涵盖不同树种（如松树、橡树、桉树）的林业过程，各类木材产品（如原木、能源用材、纸浆用材）的采集，木材锯切、干燥和刨削，工程木制品（如纤维板、胶合层积材、胶合板）生产，木材防腐剂生产及不同防腐方法的应用，以及木颗粒燃料生产。该行业还包括林业所用机械生产的数据集，但不包括肥料生产数据。

地理覆盖包括欧洲（RER）及其部分国家（德国（DE）、葡萄牙（PT）、瑞典（SE）、瑞士（CH）），亚洲的部分国家（中国（CN）、马来西亚（MY）、泰国（TH）、菲律宾（PH）），以及美洲的部分国家或地区（巴西（BR），包括其部分地区；加拿大魁北克（CA-QC）；哥伦比亚（CO）；厄瓜多尔（EC））。

### 行业重点 <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

#### 林业 <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库中的林业过程建模涵盖混交林和九种不同针叶与阔叶树种的数据，包括松树、云杉、橡树、桉树，以及红铁木（azobe）、柳桉（meranti）等异域木材树种。此外，数据库还包含软木和竹林经营数据。所覆盖的林业过程主要是可持续经营的森林。对于桉树，还包括人工林经营数据集。

数据库涵盖林业全过程，包括树苗生产、造林、森林经营、树木采伐，以及最终生产原木、纸浆用材和能源用材等不同林产品。

林业活动所用肥料的生产数据包含在[化学品与塑料行业](chemicals-and-plastics.md)中。

#### 锯木厂 <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库中的锯木厂运行包括去皮、针叶材和阔叶材锯切，以及随后对锯材进行干燥和刨削。数据涵盖不同干燥技术（空气干燥和窑干）及不同锯材产品（梁材、板材和板条）。数据库还包含锯木厂活动中产生的各种副产品，例如树皮、木片、锯屑、刨花、板皮和边板（slab and siding）。这些副产品部分被切成木片，或直接作为其他活动的投入。

#### 工程木制品 <a href="#zh-b0013" id="zh-b0013"></a>

数据库涵盖各种工程木制品的数据，包括胶合层积材、胶合板、定向刨花板和各类纤维板等。它还提供生产工程木制品常用树脂与胶黏剂的数据，例如三聚氰胺脲醛胶黏剂和聚氨酯胶黏剂。除木质板材外，还提供竹质建筑材料的数据集，例如胶合层积竹材和编织竹席板。

板材生产所产生废水的处理数据包含在[废物管理与回收利用行业](waste-management-and-recycling.md)中。

#### 木材防腐 <a href="#zh-b0016" id="zh-b0016"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库以高度详尽的方式表示木材防腐剂生产及其应用数据。数据库包括溶剂型与水性防腐剂、有机盐型防腐剂（不含铬）、无机盐型防腐剂（含铬）以及杂酚油（creosote）生产数据。

此外，还提供各种木材防腐方法的信息，包括加压法（真空加压法、冷热槽浸渍法、振荡加压法、压力罐法）和非加压法（浸蘸与浸泡法、自动喷涂法和浇淋法）。

#### 木材焚烧 <a href="#zh-b0019" id="zh-b0019"></a>

数据库提供利用锯木过程残余物（刨花、锯屑、木片）生产木颗粒燃料的数据。利用木颗粒、木片和原木产热的各种数据集可在[能源行业](energy.md)中获取。

#### 基础设施 <a href="#zh-b0021" id="zh-b0021"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库包含多种全球层面的木材与林业行业基础设施（设备、机械、设施）数据集，包括林业机械（集材拖拉机、架空集材机、集运机、采伐机和削片机），锯木活动相关基础设施（锯木厂、技术性木材干燥设施），木颗粒及各类木质板材工厂（即水泥胶结板和有机胶结板），以及木材防腐设施、炉窑和除尘过滤器的各种数据集。

### <strong>数据</strong><strong>提供者</strong> <a href="#zh-b0023" id="zh-b0023"></a>

ecoinvent 数据来自不同来源。林业与木材行业生命周期清单（LCI）数据集的主要提供者包括但不限于：

- [巴西农业研究公司](https://www.embrapa.br/)（EMBRAPA）
- [产品、服务和系统生命周期国际参考中心](https://ciraig.org/)（CIRAIG）

## The forestry and wood sector is a sector of human activity focused on harvesting and processing wood for various applications. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

April 10th, 2024

[60% of global forests](https://www.un.org/esa/forests/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/UNFF13_BkgdStudy_ForestsSCP.pdf) are primarily or partially used for wood and non-wood products. In the ecoinvent database, the sector comprises roughly 800 datasets. These include forestry processes for various species (e.g., pine, oak, eucalyptus), extraction of various wood products (e.g., logs, energy wood, pulpwood), sawing, drying, and planning of wood, production of engineered wood products (e.g., fibreboard, glued laminated timber, plywood), production of wood preservatives, application of different preservation methods, and the production of wood pellets. Datasets related to machinery production used for forestry are also included in the sector. Data on fertiliser production are not included in the sector.

Geographies covered include Europe (RER) and some countries within Europe (Germany (DE), Portugal (PT), Sweden (SE), Switzerland (CH)), some countries in Asia (China (CN), Malaysia (MY), Thailand (TH), the Philippines (PH)), and some countries in the Americas (Brazil (BR), including some of its territories, Canada, Québec (CA-QC), Colombia (CO), and Ecuador (EC)).

### Sector Highlights <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

#### Forestry <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

The modeling of forestry processes in the ecoinvent database covers data on forests of mixed species and for nine different softwood and hardwood species. Those include pine, spruce, oak, eucalyptus as well as exotic woods such as azobe and meranti, among others. Furthermore the database also includes data on cork and bamboo forestry. Forestry processes are covered mainly as sustainably managed forests. For eucalyptus, datasets covering planted forest management are included.

The database covers the full range of processes in forestry. These include tree seedling production, forests’ establishment, management, harvesting of trees, and finally, the production of different forestry products such as logs, pulpwood, and wood for energy production purposes.

Data on the production of fertilisers used in forestry activities are included in the [chemicals and plastics sector](chemicals-and-plastics.md).

#### Sawmill <a href="#en-b0011" id="en-b0011"></a>

Sawmill operations in the ecoinvent database include the debarking, sawing of the softwood and hardwood, and the subsequential drying and planning of the sawn wood. Data is available on different drying techniques (air drying and kiln drying), as well as on different sawn wood products (beam, board, and lath). The database also includes various byproducts produced during the sawmill activities, such as bark and wood chips, sawdust, shavings, and slab and siding, which are partly chipped into wood chips or used directly as an input in other activities.

#### Engineered Wood Products <a href="#en-b0013" id="en-b0013"></a>

The database covers data on various engineered wood products including, among others, glued laminated timber, plywood, oriented strand board, and different types of fiberboard. It also offers data on commonly used resins and adhesives used to produce engineered wood products, such as melamine urea formaldehyde adhesive and polyurethane adhesive. Next to the wood-based boards, datasets on bamboo-based construction materials are available, such as glued laminated bamboo and woven bamboo mat panels.

Data for the treatment of wastewater resulting from board production are included in the [waste management and recycling sector](waste-management-and-recycling.md).

#### Wood Preservation <a href="#en-b0016" id="en-b0016"></a>

Data on the production of wood preservatives and their application are represented at a high level of detail in the ecoinvent database. The database includes data on the production of solvent and water-based preservatives, organic salt-based preservatives (chrome-free), inorganic salt-based preservatives (chrome-containing), and the production of creosote.

In addition, information on various methods of wood preservation is available, including pressure methods (vacuum pressure process, hot and cold dipping process oscillating pressure process, pressure vessel process) and non-pressure methods (dipping and immersion process, automated spraying, and deluging process).

#### Wood Incineration <a href="#en-b0019" id="en-b0019"></a>

The database provides data on the production of wood pellets from residues of the sawmill processes (shavings, sawdust, woodchips). Various datasets on heat production from wood pellets, as well as woodchips and logs are available in the [energy sector](energy.md).

#### Infrastructure <a href="#en-b0021" id="en-b0021"></a>

The ecoinvent database contains various datasets on a global level pertaining to the infrastructure of the wood and forestry sector (equipment, machinery, facilities). These include datasets for forestry machinery (skidders, yarders, forwarders, harvesters, and chippers), for infrastructure related to sawmill activities (sawmill, technical wood drying facility), for wood pellet and different types of wooden board factories (i.e., for cement bonded boards and organic bonded boards) as well as various datasets for wood preservation facilities, furnaces, and dust filters.

### <strong>D</strong><strong>ata Providers</strong> <a href="#en-b0023" id="en-b0023"></a>

ecoinvent data originates from different sources. Notable providers of LCI datasets for the forestry and wood sector include (but are not limited to):

- [Brazilian Agricultural Research Corporation](https://www.embrapa.br/) (EMBRAPA)
- [International Reference Center for Life Cycle of Products, Services and Systems](https://ciraig.org/) (CIRAIG)
