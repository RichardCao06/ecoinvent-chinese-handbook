### <strong>生化需氧量（BOD）</strong> <a href="#zh-b0005" id="zh-b0005"></a>

在一定温度和规定时间内，好氧分解者分解给定体积水中的有机物所需的溶解氧量。

### <strong>BOD</strong> <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

### <strong>Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD)</strong> <a href="#en-b0005" id="en-b0005"></a>

Amount of dissolved oxygen needed by aerobic decomposers to break down the organic materials in a given volume of water at a certain temperature over a specified time period.

### <strong>BOD</strong> <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

Abbreviation for biological oxygen demand

### <strong>By-Product / Waste</strong> <a href="#en-b0009" id="en-b0009"></a>

Any activity output that is neither a reference product nor an exchange to the environment.
