## ecoinvent 数据库的构建方式可支持多种环境标准。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2024 年 11 月 18 日

如今，涉及不同行业部门和地理区域的各类标准规定要求、指南和特性，以确保材料、产品、过程和服务适合其用途。不同部门有多种环境评价相关标准。遵循这些标准可确保相关评价和报告的可信度与一致性。

ecoinvent 数据库旨在利用透明、高质量的数据，支持成本可承受且易于开展的环境评价。为实现这一目标，ecoinvent 数据库的结构遵循下列环境标准。

### 标准清单 <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

ecoinvent 数据可支持旨在遵循下列标准的环境评价和报告。

#### 环境管理 ISO14000 标准族 <a href="#zh-b0008" id="zh-b0008"></a>

ISO14000 标准族涵盖一系列环境管理相关标准，包括环境管理体系、环境标志、环境绩效评价、生命周期评价，以及温室气体管理及相关活动的标准。

在此查看全部 ISO 标准：

[ISO/TC 207/SC 1：环境管理体系。](https://www.iso.org/committee/54818/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 3：环境标志](https://www.iso.org/committee/54836/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 4：环境绩效评价](https://www.iso.org/committee/54846/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 5：生命周期评价](https://www.iso.org/committee/54854/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 7：温室气体管理](https://www.iso.org/committee/546318/x/catalogue/)

#### 用于环境产品声明（EPD）的 EN15804 <a href="#zh-b0016" id="zh-b0016"></a>

EN15804 是一项欧洲标准，规定环境产品声明（EPD）的核心规则。

参见 [EN15804](https://www.en-standard.eu/csn-en-15804-a2-sustainability-of-construction-works-environmental-product-declarations-core-rules-for-the-product-category-of-construction-products/)

#### 用于环境产品声明（EPD）的 ISO21930 <a href="#zh-b0019" id="zh-b0019"></a>

ISO21930 在国际层面规定环境产品声明（EPD）的核心规则。

参见[建筑和土木工程可持续性](https://www.iso.org/committee/322621/x/catalogue/)相关 ISO 标准。

#### ISCC 认证 <a href="#zh-b0022" id="zh-b0022"></a>

ISCC 是针对生物质和生物燃料（燃料及电力）的国际认证体系，规定认证规则和程序。

参见 [ISCC 认证](https://www.iscc-system.org/)

#### GHG Protocol《企业价值链（范围 3）标准》 <a href="#zh-b0025" id="zh-b0025"></a>

温室气体排放清单规范，包含价值链活动产生的间接排放。

参见[《企业价值链（范围 3）标准》](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-value-chain-scope-3-standard)

#### ILCD 手册 <a href="#zh-b0028" id="zh-b0028"></a>

一组符合 LCA 国际标准（ISO 14040/44）的文档。

ILCD 手册由欧盟委员会联合研究中心（JRC）的环境与可持续发展研究所与环境总司合作编制。

参见 [ILCD 手册](https://eplca.jrc.ec.europa.eu/?page_id=86)

## The ecoinvent database is constructed in a way to support various environmental standards. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Nov 18th, 2024

Nowadays, different standards relating to diverse industrial sectors and geographies define requirements, guidelines, and characteristics that ensure materials, products, processes, and services fit their purpose. There are various standards related to environmental assessments in various sectors. Compliance with such standards ensures the credibility and consistency of such assessments and reports.

The purpose of the ecoinvent database is to support affordable, easy environmental assessments with transparent, high-quality data. To fulfill this goal, the ecoinvent database is structured to respect the environmental standards listed below.

### List of Standards <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

ecoinvent data can be used to support environmental assessments and reports that aim to comply to the following standards.

#### ISO14000 Family for Environmental Management <a href="#en-b0008" id="en-b0008"></a>

The ISO14000 family covers a series of standards related to environmental management. These include standards on Environmental Management Systems, Environmental Labelling, Environmental Performance Evaluation, Life Cycle Assessment, and Greenhouse Gas Management and related activities.

See all ISO Standards here:

[ISO/TC 207/SC 1 on Environmental Management Systems.](https://www.iso.org/committee/54818/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 3 on Environmental Labelling](https://www.iso.org/committee/54836/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 4 on Environmental Performance Evaluation](https://www.iso.org/committee/54846/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 5 on Life Cycle Assessment](https://www.iso.org/committee/54854/x/catalogue/)

[ISO/TC 207/SC 7 on Greenhouse Gas Management](https://www.iso.org/committee/546318/x/catalogue/)

#### EN15804 for Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) <a href="#en-b0016" id="en-b0016"></a>

The EN15804 is a European standard that sets the core rules for Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs).

See [EN15804](https://www.en-standard.eu/csn-en-15804-a2-sustainability-of-construction-works-environmental-product-declarations-core-rules-for-the-product-category-of-construction-products/)

#### ISO21930 for Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) <a href="#en-b0019" id="en-b0019"></a>

ISO21930 sets the core rules for Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) at an international level.

See ISO Standards on [Sustainability in Buildings and Civil Engineering Works](https://www.iso.org/committee/322621/x/catalogue/)

#### ISCC Certifications <a href="#en-b0022" id="en-b0022"></a>

ISCC is an international certification system for Biomass and Biofuels (fuels and electricity) that describes the rules and procedures for certification.

See the [ISCC Certification](https://www.iscc-system.org/)

#### GHG Protocol, Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard <a href="#en-b0025" id="en-b0025"></a>

Specification for greenhouse gas emissions inventory including indirect emissions resulting from value chain activities.

See the [Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard](https://ghgprotocol.org/corporate-value-chain-scope-3-standard)

#### ILCD Handbook <a href="#en-b0028" id="en-b0028"></a>

A set of documents that are in line with the international standards on LCA (ISO 14040/44).

The ILCD handbook was developed by the Institute for Environment and Sustainability in the European Commission Joint Research Centre (JRC), in co-operation with the Environment DG.

See the [ILCD Handbook](https://eplca.jrc.ec.europa.eu/?page_id=86)
