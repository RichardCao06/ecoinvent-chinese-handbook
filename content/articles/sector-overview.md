## ecoinvent 数据库涵盖广泛的工业行业。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2025 年 11 月 5 日

ecoinvent 数据库是一个全球性的人类活动资料库，记录各工业行业供应商品和服务的数千种过程。这些活动既消耗来自环境的自然资源，也消耗其他人类活动的产品。同时，它们向水、土壤和空气排放物质。  
  
具体而言，ecoinvent 数据库包含超过 26,000 个可靠的生命周期清单数据集，涵盖众多工业行业，包括农业和畜牧业、建筑与施工、化学品与塑料、能源、林业与木材、金属、纺织品、运输、旅游住宿、废物处理与回收利用以及供水等。

## The ecoinvent database covers a wide range of industrial sectors. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Nov 5th, 2025

The ecoinvent database is a global library of human activities, representing thousands of processes that supply goods and services across all industrial sectors. These activities consume both natural resources from the environment, as well as products from other human activities. At the same time, they release emissions to water, soil, and air.   
  
Specifically, the ecoinvent database contains more than 26,000 reliable life cycle inventory datasets, covering a range of industrial sectors. These include agriculture and animal husbandry, building and construction, chemicals and plastics, energy, forestry and wood, metals, textiles, transport, tourist accommodation, waste treatment and recycling, and water supply, among others.
