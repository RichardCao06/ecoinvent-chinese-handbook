## 环境产品声明传达有关产品及其应用的可验证、准确且不具误导性的环境信息。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2023 年 9 月 29 日

其所提供信息的标准化有助于基于科学的决策。ISO 14025、ISO 21930 以及修订后的 EN15804 标准（EN15804&A2:2019）规定了 EPD 信息报告的规则。

EPD 提供按模块组织的信息。这些信息涉及：

- 描述资源使用、废物类别和输出流的清单指标
- 生物源碳含量信息
- 生命周期影响评价指标

## Environmental Product Declarations communicate verifiable, accurate, non-misleading environmental information for products and their applications. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Sept 29th, 2023

The standardization in the information they provide supports scientifically-based decision making. ISO 14025, ISO 21930, and the amended EN15804 standard (EN15804&A2:2019) provide the rules for reporting information in EPDs.

An EPD provides information organized in modules. This information pertains to:

- inventory indicators describing resource use, waste categories, and output flows
- information on biogenic carbon content
- life cycle impact assessment indicators

The ecoinvent version 3 database offers the "Allocation, cut-off, EN15804" system model, dedicated to support the needs of EPD practitioners. Read more [here](system-models.md) about the "Allocation, cut-off, EN15804" system model and how it supports EPD creation.

Explore [ECO Platform](https://www.eco-platform.org/epd-data.html) for numerous EPDs created using ecoinvent as a background database. Some examples are EPDs for:
