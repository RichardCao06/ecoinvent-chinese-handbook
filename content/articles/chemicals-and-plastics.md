## ecoinvent 数据库中的化学品与塑料行业包含超过 3,000 个数据集，涵盖大宗化学品和专用化学品的生产及寿命终结处理。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

#### 具体而言，该行业数据涵盖约 750 种独特产品，包括超过 450 种基础化学品、95 种塑料和橡胶，以及 83 种农用化学品。 <a href="#zh-b0003" id="zh-b0003"></a>

2025 年 1 月 17 日

化学品和塑料产品广泛用于支撑日常生活的众多工业行业。根据[IEA](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/industry/chemicals)，化学工业是全球最大的能源消费行业，其中约 50% 为原料用途。在欧盟层面，根据[Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/-/ddn-20230512-1)，这一领先地位同样存在，该行业占工业最终能源使用的 21.5%。如果当前需求增长趋势持续，联合国环境规划署（UNEP）估计，到 2030 年化学品产量将比 2017 年翻一番（[UNEP 报告](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/un-report-urgent-action-needed-tackle-chemical-pollution-global)），到 2050 年塑料生产和消费将消耗全球石油的 20%（[《全球化学品展望 II》](https://wedocs.unep.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.11822/28113/GCOII.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y)，[《一次性塑料》](https://wedocs.unep.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.11822/25496/singleUsePlastic_sustainability.pdf)）。关于直接 CO<sub>2</sub>排放，根据[IEA](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/industry/chemicals)，化学工业在 2022 年排放约 935 Mt，是全球第三大工业排放行业。因此，[欧洲化学工业](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/chemicals_en)及其他地区当前的举措，均致力于改善这一关键经济行业的可持续发展表现。

### <strong>行业</strong> <strong>概览</strong> <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库中的化学品与塑料行业包含超过 3,000 个数据集，涵盖广泛的物质，以满足不同工业行业的各种需求。数据库提供从大宗商品到高度专业化产品的数据。该行业的主要原材料来自炼油厂，在[燃料行业](fuels.md)中介绍。化学品与塑料行业再将这些炼油产品转化为多种物质，例如大宗化学品、精细化学品和聚合物，它们是现代世界的重要支柱。

覆盖的地理区域包括全球（GLO）、欧洲（RER）及选定欧洲国家、美国（US）、加拿大（CA）、智利（CL）、摩洛哥（MA）、印度（IN）、中国（CN）和澳大利亚（AU）。

为支持全面建模，ecoinvent 数据库包含若干基础设施数据集，从通用化学品和塑料加工工厂到氢氧化铝、甲醇等专门工厂。此外，还提供压缩制冷系统、注塑和挤出等若干服务的数据。

数据库中的物质以纯物质建模。一个例外是按不同等级生产的化学品，例如硅（电子级、冶金级和太阳能级）。如果评价需要稀释状态物质的数据，应在必要时向评价中添加所需的水或溶剂量。为方便 ecoinvent 数据用户进行这一操作，数据库中某些化学品的活动名称包含工业上最常用的溶液状态。

### <strong>行业</strong> <strong>重点</strong> <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

#### 肥料和农药 <a href="#zh-b0012" id="zh-b0012"></a>

化学品行业包含以纯物质形式制造化学品的数据集，而化学品作为肥料和农药施用的数据集则纳入[农业部门](agriculture-fishery-and-animal-husbandry.md)。ecoinvent 数据库覆盖五大洲近二十个地理区域。

具体而言，ecoinvent 数据库提供多种农药和肥料，涵盖氨、尿素、硝酸铵、硝酸钾、硝酸铵钙和过磷酸盐等肥料主要有效物质的数据集。此外，数据库还包含适用于不同作物的各种有害生物防治物质的生产数据集，包括杀软体动物剂、杀菌剂、除草剂和杀虫剂。

#### 油墨和涂料 <a href="#zh-b0015" id="zh-b0015"></a>

该行业包含众多涂料和油墨的数据，包括印刷油墨、碳粉、丙烯酸清漆和醇酸涂料等。除制造数据集外，还包括部分产品的处置数据集。

#### <strong>塑料和橡胶</strong> <a href="#zh-b0017" id="zh-b0017"></a>

除化学物质外，数据库还包含多种塑料与橡胶及其在不同领域应用的信息，例如包装、[建筑与施工](building-and-construction.md)、[纺织品](textiles.md)、消费品、[运输](transport.md)、电气与电子以及工业机械。数据集包含常见塑料和橡胶（包括 PE、PET、PU、尼龙和合成橡胶）的制造与回收信息，也包括塑料回收和其他废物处理活动。

#### 清洁剂 <a href="#zh-b0019" id="zh-b0019"></a>

数据库包含聚焦清洁耗材类消费品的数据集，包括表面活性剂、漂白剂、肥皂、洗衣剂和洗碗剂。

#### 大宗及专用化学品 <a href="#zh-b0021" id="zh-b0021"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库提供近 1,500 个全球覆盖的大宗化学品数据集。具体涵盖广泛物质，包括单质、溶剂、酸碱、催化剂和工业试剂，既包括有机物，也包括无机物。

## The chemicals and plastics sector in the ecoinvent database comprises over 3,000 datasets, covering the production and end-of-life treatment of bulk chemicals as well as specialty chemicals. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

#### Namely, the sectoral data encompasses approximately 750 unique products, including over 450 basic chemicals, 95 plastics and rubber types, and 83 agrochemicals. <a href="#en-b0003" id="en-b0003"></a>

Jan 17th, 2025

Chemical and plastic products are widely used in many industrial sectors that support our daily lives. The chemical sector is the largest energy consumer worldwide, based on [IEA](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/industry/chemicals), from which approximately 50% represents use of feedstock. At the European Union level, based on [eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/-/ddn-20230512-1), the latter precedence persists as the sector accounts for 21.5% of the final industrial energy use. Should the current trends of growing demand continue, UNEP estimates the production of chemicals will have doubled by 2030 compared to 2017 figures ([UNEP Report](https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/press-release/un-report-urgent-action-needed-tackle-chemical-pollution-global)) and the production and consumption of plastic to consume 20% of global oil by 2050 ([Global Chemicals Outlook II](https://wedocs.unep.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.11822/28113/GCOII.pdf?sequence=1&isAllowed=y), [Single-use plastics](https://wedocs.unep.org/bitstream/handle/20.500.11822/25496/singleUsePlastic_sustainability.pdf)). Regarding direct CO<sub>2</sub> emissions, based on [IEA](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/industry/chemicals), the chemical sector released about 935 Mt in 2022, and is classified as the third largest industrial emitter worldwide. Hence, current initiatives from the [European chemical industry](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/chemicals_en) and beyond focus on improving the sustainability performance of this key economic sector.

### <strong>Sector</strong> <strong>Overview</strong> <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

The chemicals and plastics sector in the ecoinvent database comprises over 3,000 datasets, covering a broad spectrum of substances that address different needs in various industrial sectors. The database accommodates data ranging from commodities to highly specialized products. The primary raw materials of this sector are sourced in refineries and are discussed in the [fuels sector](fuels.md). The chemicals and plastics sector, in turn, transforms these refinery products into a myriad of substances, e.g. from bulk to fine chemicals and polymers, that are a pivotal pillar of our modern world.

The geographies covered are Global (GLO), Europe (RER), and selected European countries, United States (US), Canada (CA), Chile (CL), Morocco (MA), India (IN), China (CN), and Australia (AU).

To allow holistic modeling, the ecoinvent database includes several infrastructure datasets, ranging from generic chemical and plastic processing factories to specialized plants such as aluminium hydroxide and methanol factories, among others. Additionally, data about several services such as compression refrigeration systems, injection moulding, and extrusion are available.

Substances in the database are modeled as pure substances. An exception refers to chemicals that are produced in different grades, e.g. silicon (electronics, metallurgical, and solar grade). If an assessment demands data on substances in a diluted state, the required amount of water or solvent should be added to the assessment where necessary. To facilitate this for ecoinvent data users, the activity names in the ecoinvent database include the industrially most applied solution state for selected chemicals.

### <strong>Sector</strong> <strong>Highlights</strong> <a href="#en-b0011" id="en-b0011"></a>

#### Fertilisers and Pesticides <a href="#en-b0012" id="en-b0012"></a>

While datasets on the manufacturing of chemicals as pure substances are included in the chemicals sector, datasets referring to chemicals applied as fertilisers and pesticides are integrated into the [agriculture sector](agriculture-fishery-and-animal-husbandry.md). The ecoinvent database covers nearly twenty geographies across five continents.

Specifically, the ecoinvent database accommodates a wide variety of pesticides and fertilisers. Datasets of the main active substances in fertilisers, such as ammonia, urea, ammonium nitrate, potassium nitrate, calcium ammonium nitrate, and superphosphates are covered. Further, the database includes datasets on production of various pest-controlling substances, including molluscicides, fungicides, herbicides, and insecticides applicable to various crops.

#### Ink and Paints <a href="#en-b0015" id="en-b0015"></a>

This sector includes data on numerous paints and inks, covering printing inks, toners, acrylic varnish, and alkyd paint among others. In addition to datasets on manufacturing, datasets on the disposal of selected products are also included.

#### <strong>Plastics and Rubber</strong> <a href="#en-b0017" id="en-b0017"></a>

Besides the coverage of chemical substances, the database includes information about a wide variety of plastics and rubbers and their use in different sectors, such as packaging, [building and construction](building-and-construction.md), [textiles](textiles.md), consumer products, [transportation](transport.md), electrical and electronics, and industrial machinery. Datasets include information about the manufacturing and recycling of common plastics and rubbers (including PE, PET, PU, nylon, and synthetic rubber). Plastic recycling and other waste treatment activities are included.

#### Cleaning Agents <a href="#en-b0019" id="en-b0019"></a>

The database includes datasets focused on various consumer products used as cleaning consumables. These include tensides, bleaches, soap, laundry, and dishwashing detergents.

#### Bulk and Specialized Chemicals <a href="#en-b0021" id="en-b0021"></a>

The ecoinvent database offers nearly 1,500 datasets on bulk chemicals with worldwide coverage. Specifically, it includes a broad spectrum of substances, including elemental substances, solvents, acids and bases, catalysts, and industrially used reagents, both organic and inorganic.
