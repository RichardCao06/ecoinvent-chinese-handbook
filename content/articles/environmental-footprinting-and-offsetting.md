##

Sept 29th, 2023

Calculators of carbon footprints rely on background databases to compute CO2 emissions associated with transport, energy consumptions and goods. However, the accuracy and credibility of these assessments rely on the availability of relevant, high-quality data.

ecoinvent compiles and reviews the best available environmental data out there.

Explore some examples of companies using ecoinvent data for Environmental Footprinting and Offsetting:
