##

2023 年 9 月 29 日

企业可持续发展报告（CSR）是实现可持续经济的基础。它要求企业分享更有针对性、可靠且易于获取的信息，作为可持续决策的依据。

例如，GRI 标准帮助组织理解其对外部的影响，即对经济、环境和社会的影响。这增强了组织对其可持续发展贡献的问责性和透明度。

国际公认的 ecoinvent 数据库中的排放因子用于每年编制企业生态平衡表和企业可持续发展报告。

##

Sept 29th, 2023

Corporate Sustainability Reporting (CSR) is fundamental for achieving a sustainable economy. It requires companies to share more targeted, reliable, and easily accessible information as the basis for sustainable decision-making.

For example, the GRI Standards help organizations understand their outward impacts: on the economy, environment, and society. This increases accountability and enhances transparency on their contribution to sustainable development.

Emission factors from the internationally recognized ecoinvent database are used for the annual preparation of corporate eco-balance and CSR.
