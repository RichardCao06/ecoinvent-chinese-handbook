### LCA <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

生命周期评价（Life Cycle Assessment）的缩写。

### LCI <a href="#zh-b0013" id="zh-b0013"></a>

生命周期清单（Life Cycle Inventory）的缩写。

### LCIA <a href="#zh-b0015" id="zh-b0015"></a>

生命周期影响评价（Life Cycle Impact Assessment）的缩写。

### LCIA 结果值 <a href="#zh-b0017" id="zh-b0017"></a>

生命周期影响评价结果值的简称。一个 LCIA 结果值指一个数据集的一个 LCIA 指标，例如“mango production, BR”（巴西芒果生产）的 IPCC2013 100 年全球变暖潜势（GWP）指标。如果考虑“mango production, BR”的 IPCC2013 GWP100a 和 EF3.0 土地利用（土壤质量指数），则一个数据集对应两个指标，即两个 LCIA 结果值。

### 生命周期评价（LCA） <a href="#zh-b0019" id="zh-b0019"></a>

对产品系统在整个生命周期中的输入、输出及潜在环境影响进行汇总和评价（ISO 1440:2006，原文标准编号）。

### 生命周期影响评价（LCIA） <a href="#zh-b0021" id="zh-b0021"></a>

生命周期评价中旨在理解和评价产品系统在产品整个生命周期内潜在环境影响的大小及重要性的阶段（ISO 14040:2006）。

### 生命周期清单（LCI） <a href="#zh-b0023" id="zh-b0023"></a>

生命周期评价中汇总并量化产品在整个生命周期中的输入和输出的阶段（ISO 14040:2006）。

### 产品寿命 <a href="#zh-b0025" id="zh-b0025"></a>

从产品生产到开始对其进行废物处理之间的时间。

### 链接 <a href="#zh-b0027" id="zh-b0027"></a>

ecoinvent 第 3 版数据库包含单元过程层面的未链接数据集（UPR）。这些未链接数据集可以采用不同系统模型，通过市场进行链接。

### LCIA Score <a href="#en-b0017" id="en-b0017"></a>

Abbreviation for Life Cycle Impact Assessment score. One LCIA score is one LCIA indicator for one dataset, for example IPCC2013 Global Warming Potential (GWP) 100a for “mango production, BR.” If you consider IPCC2013 GWP100a and EF3.0 land use (soil quality index) for “mango production, BR,” this counts as two indicators for one dataset, i.e., two LCIA scores.

### Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) <a href="#en-b0019" id="en-b0019"></a>

The compilation and evaluation of the inputs, outputs and the potential environmental impacts of a product system throughout its life cycle (ISO 1440:2006).

### Life Cycle Impact Assessment (LCIA) <a href="#en-b0021" id="en-b0021"></a>

The phase of life cycle assessment aimed at understanding and evaluating the magnitude and significance of the potential environmental impacts for a product system throughout the life cycle of the product (ISO 14040:2006).

### Life Cycle Inventory (LCI) <a href="#en-b0023" id="en-b0023"></a>

The phase of life cycle assessment involving the compilation and quantification of inputs and outputs for a product throughout its life cycle (ISO 14040:2006).

### Lifetime of a Product <a href="#en-b0025" id="en-b0025"></a>

The period between the time of production and the time of initiating waste treatment of the product.

### Linking <a href="#en-b0027" id="en-b0027"></a>

The ecoinvent version 3 database contains unlinked datasets on unit process level (UPR). These unlinked datasets can be linked through the markets using different system models.
