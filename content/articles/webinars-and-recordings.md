## 观看围绕 ecoinvent 数据库各种主题的网络研讨会。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2025 年 12 月 10 日

我们的网络研讨会旨在帮助 ecoinvent 数据库用户理解和使用数据库。主题主要涉及 ecoinvent 数据全貌、数据库的结构与方法学，以及用户常见问题。

所有网络研讨会均免费，可供 100 人参加。参加任何网络研讨会均需提前注册。即将举办的网络研讨会请参阅 ecoinvent 网站。

## Watch ecoinvent webinars on various topics around the ecoinvent database. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Dec 10th, 2025

Our webinars aim to support users of the ecoinvent database in their understanding and use of the ecoinvent database. The topics are focused on the ecoinvent data landscape, the structure and methodology of the ecoinvent database, as well as on common questions of ecoinvent users.

All our webinars are free of charge, enabling the participation of 100 people. For participation in any of our webinars, registration in advance is required. See the ecoinvent website for upcoming webinars.

### <strong>Topics</strong> <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

#### <strong>ecoinvent Vision</strong> <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

The future of ecoinvent is the future of environmental data. In this webinar, our team introduces our roadmap, explaining how we will achieve our next transformation.
