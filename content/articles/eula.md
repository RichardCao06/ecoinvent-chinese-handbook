##

2023年9月27日

### <strong>我们使用 ecoinvent 数据库计算了某款产品的碳足迹。是否可以使用该碳足迹，例如用于广告？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0003" id="zh-b0003"></a>

您计算的碳足迹在[EULA](https://ecoinvent.org/gdpr-eula/)中称为“计算所得数据”（Calculated Data）。允许以静态方式与第三方（即非许可证持有者）共享计算所得数据，例如通过 PDF 报告或 Word 文档。不允许通过工具或应用等自动化方式与第三方共享计算结果。

### <strong>能否定义 EULA“限制”（Restrictions）条款中的“工具”（Tools），并提供一些例子？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0005" id="zh-b0005"></a>

“工具”可以是任何软件工具、软件即服务（SaaS）平台或基于网页的工具。例如，供公司内部使用的基于 Excel 的碳足迹计算器、提供给客户用于比较不同产品排放因子的网页工具，或允许客户自行配置产品的网上商店等。

### <strong>谁需要签署 ecoinvent EULA？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

- 对于单用户：使用 ecoinvent 数据库的个人（自然人、被许可人）需要签署／接受[EULA](https://ecoinvent.org/gdpr-eula/)。
- 对于机构／实体：适用该实体的签署权限规定，即许可证负责人（例如教授）签署[EULA](https://ecoinvent.org/gdpr-eula/)，并对机构／实体内约定用户范围中其他符合资格用户的使用负责。

### <strong>一个人（被许可人）是否可以在多台计算机上使用单用户许可证？是否允许远程访问，例如通过 VPN？ </strong> <a href="#zh-b0010" id="zh-b0010"></a>

可以。单用户许可证的被许可人可以使用多台计算机。也允许远程访问，但仅限被许可人本人使用。

### <strong>教育多用户许可证的符合资格用户（教授所指导的学生）能否通过多台计算机访问 ecoinvent 数据库？是否允许远程访问，例如通过 VPN？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0012" id="zh-b0012"></a>

可以。教育多用户许可证的所有符合资格用户可以从大学场所内登记的不同计算机访问 ecoinvent 数据库。如果用于访问的计算机位于被许可人的大学场所内，则允许远程访问。

### <strong>单用户许可证的被许可人可以与其他人共用其计算机吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0014" id="zh-b0014"></a>

除非其他人也持有单用户许可证，否则非被许可人不能使用被许可人的计算机。

### <strong>教育多用户许可证的被许可人可以与其他人共用其计算机吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0016" id="zh-b0016"></a>

除非其他人属于符合资格用户（教授所指导的学生），否则非被许可人不能使用被许可人的计算机。

### <strong>We Calculated the Carbon Footprint for One of Our Products Using the ecoinvent Database. Are We Allowed to Use This Carbon Footprint, e.g., for Advertising?</strong> <a href="#en-b0003" id="en-b0003"></a>

The carbon footprint you calculated is referred to as ‘Calculated Data’ in the [EULA](https://ecoinvent.org/gdpr-eula/). It is allowed to share Calculated Data with third parties (=non-license holders) statically (for example, in a PDF report/ Word document). It is not allowed to share the results of calculations with third parties in an automated way (for example, through a tool or an application).

### <strong>Could You Please Define and Provide some Examples of "Tools" as They Are Used Under "Restrictions" in the EULA?</strong> <a href="#en-b0005" id="en-b0005"></a>

A “tool” could be any software tool, Software as a Service (SaaS) platform, or web-based tool. For example, an Excel-based carbon footprint calculator for internal use inside a company, a web tool for comparing the emission factors of different products available to clients, an e-Shop where customers can build their own product, etc.

### <strong>Who Needs to Sign the ecoinvent EULA? </strong> <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

- For single users: the [EULA](https://ecoinvent.org/gdpr-eula/) needs to be signed/accepted by an individual (person, licensee) who’s using the ecoinvent database.
- For institutions/entities: the signing authority of such entity applies, i.e., the responsible person for the license (e.g., a professor) signs the [EULA](https://ecoinvent.org/gdpr-eula/) and is responsible for the use of other eligible users of the license according to the agreed user base within the institution/entity.

### <strong>Can One Person (Licensee) Use a Single-User License on Several Computers? And Is Remote Access (e.g. Through a VPN) Possible? </strong> <a href="#en-b0010" id="en-b0010"></a>

Yes, several computers are allowed to be used by the licensee of a Single-User License. And yes, remote access is permitted if it’s used by the licensee only. 

### <strong>Can the Eligible Users (Students of a Professorship) of an Educational Multi-User License Work on Several Computers to Access the ecoinvent Database? And is Remote Access (e.g., Through a VPN) Possible?</strong> <a href="#en-b0012" id="en-b0012"></a>

Yes, all eligible users of an Educational Multi-User License can access the ecoinvent database from different computers registered at the university site. Remote access is permitted if the accessing computer is located at the university site of the licensee.

### <strong>Can a Licensee of a Single-User License Share her/his Computer With Other Individuals?</strong> <a href="#en-b0014" id="en-b0014"></a>

The licensee’s computer cannot be used by non-licensees unless the other individuals are holding a Single-User License as well.

### <strong>Can a Licensee of an Educational Multi-User License Share her/his Computer With Other Individuals?</strong> <a href="#en-b0016" id="en-b0016"></a>

The licensee’s computer cannot be used by non-licensees unless the other individuals are eligible users (students of a professorship).
