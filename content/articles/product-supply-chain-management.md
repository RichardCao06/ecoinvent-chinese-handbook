##

2023年9月29日

环境法规和立法不断增加，市场对采用可持续供应链的产品需求也在增长。这要求采用完善的产品供应链管理应用，支持根据环境绩效评价和选择材料及相应供应商。

要对可持续材料和供应商作出正确决策，产品供应链管理者需要以可靠、优质的环境数据作为基础。

ecoinvent 数据库以透明、一致的方式，涵盖产品在整个价值链中的各个阶段。

##

Sept 29th, 2023

An increasing number of environmental regulations and legislations and a larger market demand for products following sustainable supply chains require sophisticated product supply chain management applications supporting the evaluation and selection of materials and their appropriate suppliers based on their environmental performance.

To make the right decisions on sustainable materials and suppliers, product supply chain managers need to count on reliable and high-quality environmental data in the background.

The ecoinvent database considers a product’s stages alongside the entire value chain in a transparent and consistent way.

Explore this example of how ecoinvent data is used for product supply chain management:
