## ecoinvent v3.5 于 2018 年发布，更新了废物处理、硬煤和铝供应、纸浆与瓦楞纸板原纸生产，并首次引入水产养殖和捕捞数据。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

<strong>ecoinvent v3.5 于 2018 年 8 月 23 日发布</strong>。了解如何[升级到最新版本](licensing.md)。

2023年10月27日

此外，供应链的表示方式经过修订。需求活动与供应活动之间的一千多个链接得到更新，并引入了数百个涵盖不同产品的地区市场新数据集。

为确保清晰和透明，我们公开记录最新更新与此前数据库版本之间的变更。因此，我们提供多种文件，记录并解释每个数据库版本的变更，包括详细变更报告、对应关系文件，以及包含每版全部活动的文件。此外，我们记录每个版本的全部已知问题，以提醒数据库用户，并支持其理解和使用数据库。

### 新增和更新的数据 <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

#### 水产养殖与捕捞 <a href="#zh-b0008" id="zh-b0008"></a>

ecoinvent 首次发布水产养殖与捕捞数据集，新增鳀鱼、无须鳕和金枪鱼捕捞，以及鳟鱼和罗非鱼养殖相关数据集；还包含加工为鱼粉和鱼油，以及罐装、腌制和冷冻等鱼类加工活动。

#### 纸浆和瓦楞纸板原纸生产 <a href="#zh-b0010" id="zh-b0010"></a>

纸浆部门体现由欧洲纸浆行业部门协会收集的最新硫酸盐法纸浆生产一手数据。这些新数据提高了硫酸盐法纸浆供应链的细分程度。同样，欧洲瓦楞纸板供应链采用了瓦楞纸板制造商联合会的最新数据。

#### 金属——铝 <a href="#zh-b0012" id="zh-b0012"></a>

铝供应链更新采用国际铝业协会提供的最新数据，包括世界多数地区的电力生产及原铝和再生铝生产。部分活动新增地区数据集，进一步提高本部门的分辨率和透明度。

#### 废物处理 <a href="#zh-b0014" id="zh-b0014"></a>

废物部门扩展增加了 150 个处理和处置活动，包括非卫生填埋、露天焚烧和露天堆放。此外，新增 500 个针对具体国家、涵盖常见城市废物组分的处置组合，覆盖 37 个欧洲国家以及巴西、哥伦比亚、秘鲁、印度和南非。

#### 硬煤 <a href="#zh-b0016" id="zh-b0016"></a>

3.5 版覆盖全球硬煤供应链的 95% 以上。进口和市场数据集描述全球贸易流和地区硬煤组合。印度和南非煤炭开采的新数据包括不同矿山类型。矿山关键排放已更新，并在所有地理区域一致实施。

#### 持续更新的电力部门 <a href="#zh-b0018" id="zh-b0018"></a>

3.5 版涵盖 142 个国家，覆盖接近 100% 的全球发电量（以统计所涵盖的范围为准）。当前及未来预测电力组合可在国家层面、甚至更细的地区层面获取。此外，还加入了抛物槽和太阳能塔聚光太阳能发电的新数据。

### 已知问题 <a href="#zh-b0044" id="zh-b0044"></a>

此部分列出 ecoinvent 数据库 3.5 版中的全部已知数据错误。ecoinvent 团队致力于在后续更新中消除这些错误；保留已知问题部分，是为了提醒数据库用户，并支持其理解和使用数据库。

#### 数据问题 <a href="#zh-b0046" id="zh-b0046"></a>

- 航空运输数据集目前包含“ethylene oxide”（环氧乙烷）交换。这项排放不正确。ecoinvent 正在准备航空运输更新，将在下次发布中提供。
- 为现有活动创建新的地区化数据集时，部分数据集被赋予错误的 ISIC，因此可能出现相同活动名称对应两个不同 ISIC 的情况。请查看此处列出的相关数据集及其正确 ISIC。  
  请注意，此错误不影响数据库的任何数值方面：修正后，所有交换数量、LCI 和 LCIA 均保持不变。
- 三氯氧磷生产分别由“phosphoryl chloride production”和“phosphorus oxychloride production”两个活动表示，二者表示通过不同过程生产同一产品。建议用户查阅数据集的一般说明和技术说明字段，选择最适合其研究的技术。
- “styrene production”（苯乙烯生产）数据集中向水和空气的苯排放过高。在 PlasticsEurope 的支持下，已确定可完全删除数据集中的向水苯排放，并将向空气苯排放降至 2.0E-07 kg。修正后的清单在 3.8 版引入。
- “methylene diphenyl diisocyanate production”（二苯基甲烷二异氰酸酯生产，GLO 和 RER）数据集将生成的盐酸作为废物处理，这不符合实际。盐酸是有价值的副产品，由 MDI 生产者使用或销售。ecoinvent 协会建议用户采用 PlasticsEurope 网站现有[ecoProfile](https://plasticseurope.org/sustainability/circularity/life-cycle-thinking/eco-profiles-set/)的 LCIA 结果值，或者删除废物中和、将 HCl 作为副产品处理，以修正 ecoinvent 单元过程。  
  修正 ecoinvent 数据集时，建议使用 ecoQuery 中的 未指定系统模型（Undefined）的数据集，并删除氢氧化钠输入和钠、氯化物输出交换。

#### APOS 系统模型问题 <a href="#zh-b0052" id="zh-b0052"></a>

- APOS 系统模型从局部视角处理产品分类（mft 与 non-mft）。引入大量当地市场和当地供应链后，部分供应链发生失真，在某些情况下影响 LCIA 结果。如需下载完整失真评价，请查阅 ecoQuery 3.5 版文件部分的“ecoinvent 3.5\_APOS\_known issues.xlsx”。对于 RoW 的“heat production, untreated waste wood, at furnace 1000-5000 kW”和“heat production, untreated waste wood, at furnace 1000-5000 kW, state-of-the-art 2014”，如果这些活动对您的过程贡献重要，建议核查 Cut-off 系统模型。

#### Consequential 系统模型问题 <a href="#zh-b0054" id="zh-b0054"></a>

“social”（社会）区室中存在三个基本流交换。由于 ecoinvent 尚未考虑社会影响，在单元过程和清单层面均可忽略这些交换。

## ecoinvent v3.5 was released in 2018. It featured updates in waste treatment, hard coal and aluminum supply, pulp, and containerboard production and introduced data on aquaculture and fish capture for the first time. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

<strong>ecoinvent v3.5 was released on August 23rd, 2018</strong>. Discover how to [upgrade to our latest version](licensing.md).

Oct 27th, 2023

Further, the representation of supply chains was revised. Over a thousand links between demanding and supplying activities were updated and hundreds of new, regional market datasets for various products were introduced.

As we wish to ensure clarity and transparency, we openly document the changes made between the latest update and the previous version of the database. Therefore, we offer various files that document and clarify the changes that have been made with each version of the database. These include detailed reports of changes, correspondence files, as well as files that include all activities of each version. Further, we document all known issues of each version to alert database users and to support their understanding and use of the database.

### New and Updated Data <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

#### Aquaculture and Fish Capture <a href="#en-b0008" id="en-b0008"></a>

For the first time, datasets on aquaculture and fish capture are published in ecoinvent. Datasets related to anchovy, hake and tuna capture as well as trout and tilapia aquaculture, were added to the database. Processing into fish meal and oil, as well as fish processing activities, such as canning, curing and freezing, are also included.

#### Pulp Production and Containerboard <a href="#en-b0010" id="en-b0010"></a>

The pulp sector represents the latest primary data on sulfate pulp production, collected by the European Pulp Industry Sector Association. This new data provides a higher level of granularity in the sulfate pulp supply chains. Similarly, the European corrugated board supply chain features the latest data from the Federation of Corrugated Board Manufacturers.

#### Metals - Aluminium <a href="#en-b0012" id="en-b0012"></a>

The update of the aluminium supply chain features the latest data, provided by the International Aluminium Institute. This includes electricity production as well as primary and secondary aluminium production for most regions of the world. New regional datasets for some activities provide an even higher resolution and more transparency in this sector.

#### Waste Treatment <a href="#en-b0014" id="en-b0014"></a>

The expansion of the waste sector offers 150 new treatment and disposal activities, including unsanitary landfill, open burning and open dumping. Additionally, 500 new, country specific disposal mixes for common municipal waste fractions are available in 37 European countries, Brazil, Colombia, Peru, India and South Africa.

#### Hard Coal <a href="#en-b0016" id="en-b0016"></a>

Version 3.5 covers more than 95% of the global hard coal supply chains. Import and market datasets depict global trade flows and regional hard coal mixes. New data on coal mining in India and South Africa include different types of mines. Key emissions from mines were updated and consistently implemented for all geographies.

#### Continuously Updated Electricity Sector <a href="#en-b0018" id="en-b0018"></a>

Version 3.5 covers close to 100% of (statistically represented) global electricity generation, comprising 142 countries. Current and projected future electricity mixes are available on a country or even more granular, region-specific level. Moreover, new data on concentrated solar power from parabolic troughs and solar towers was added.

### Known Issues <a href="#en-b0044" id="en-b0044"></a>

This section lists all known data errors in version 3.5 of the ecoinvent database. While the ecoinvent team focuses on eliminating such errors for upcoming updates, this section of known issues exists to alert database users and to support their understanding and use of the database.

#### Data Issues <a href="#en-b0046" id="en-b0046"></a>

- The air transport datasets currently include an exchange of “ethylene oxide”. This emission is incorrect. ecoinvent is preparing an update of air transport which will be available in the next release.
- During the creation of new regionalized datasets for existing activities, the wrong ISIC was attributed to some datasets. It is therefore possible to find the same activity name associated with two different ISIC. Please find here the list of such datasets and their correct ISIC.   
  Please note that this error does not influence any numerical aspects of the database: after the correction, all exchange amounts, LCI and LCIA will remain the same.
- The production of phosphorus oxychloride is represented in two different activities “phosphoryl chloride production” and “phosphorus oxychloride production”. The two activities represent the production of the same product from different processes. We recommend users to consult the general and technology comment fields of the datasets to pick the most relevant technology for their study.
- In the dataset “styrene production”, the benzene emissions to water and to air are too high. With support of PlasticsEurope, it could be established that the benzene emissions to water can be removed completely from the dataset and the benzene emissions to air were decreased to 2.0E-07 kg. A corrected inventory was introduced in version 3.8
- The dataset “methylene diphenyl diisocyanate production” (GLO and RER) treats the formed hydrochloric acid as a waste, this does not reflect reality. Hydrochloric acid is a valuable by-product which is used or sold by the MDI producer.The ecoinvent association advices users to either use the LCIA scores from the current [ecoProfile](https://plasticseurope.org/sustainability/circularity/life-cycle-thinking/eco-profiles-set/) available on the PlasticsEurope website or to correct the unit process in ecoinvent by removing the waste neutralisation and treating HCl as a by-product.   
  To correct the ecoinvent dataset, we would advise user to use the undefined dataset available through ecoQuery and remove the exchanges of the input of sodium hydroxide and the outputs of sodium and chloride.

#### APOS System Model Issues <a href="#en-b0052" id="en-b0052"></a>

- The APOS system model handles the product classification (mft vs. non-mft) with a local perspective. With the introduction of a large quantity of local markets and local supply chains, this has created distortions of some supply chains which affects the LCIA results in certain cases. To download a full evaluation of the distortion, please consult the file “ecoinvent 3.5\_APOS\_known issues.xlsx” accessible in the file section of ecoQuery version 3.5. In the cases of the RoW “heat production, untreated waste wood, at furnace 1000-5000 kW”, and “heat production, untreated waste wood, at furnace 1000-5000 kW, state-of-the-art 2014” we advise to check the cut-off system model, when those activities are important contributors to your process.

#### Consequential System Model Issues <a href="#en-b0054" id="en-b0054"></a>

Three elementary exchanges are found in the compartment “social”. These exchanges can be ignored, both at the unit process and the inventory level, as ecoinvent does not yet account for social impacts.
