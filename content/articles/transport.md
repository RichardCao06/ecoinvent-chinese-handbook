## 目前，运输占全球能源相关温室气体排放的 23%。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2025 年 9 月 4 日

ecoinvent 数据库的运输部门包含 600 多个数据集，涵盖运输工具的生产、维护和运营、所需基础设施，以及寿命终结处理。该部门还包含冷藏运输建模数据。

目前，运输占全球能源相关温室气体排放的 23%。过去五十年中，运输排放增长超过一倍，其中 80% 的增长来自道路运输。[IPCC](https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2018/02/ipcc_wg3_ar5_chapter8.pdf)《气候变化减缓评价》（2018）指出，要减少该部门排放，关键在于使运输排放与经济增长率脱钩。

### 部门概况 <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库的运输部门包含 600 多个数据集，涵盖运输工具的生产、维护和运营、所需基础设施，以及寿命终结处理。所含数据集覆盖道路、铁路、水运、航空和管道运输。各运输方式按车辆大小、燃料、所运货物类型等相关标准细分为不同技术。此外，也提供配备冷藏运输设施的运输工具数据。

覆盖的地理区域包括全球（GLO）、欧洲（RER）及部分欧洲国家、美国（US）、中国（CN）、南非（ZA）、印度（IN）和巴西（BR）。

### 部门亮点 <a href="#zh-b0009" id="zh-b0009"></a>

#### 货物运输 <a href="#zh-b0010" id="zh-b0010"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库包含货运数据集，可对道路、铁路、内河、海洋、航空和管道货运进行建模。各运输方式均由不同技术的数据集表示。道路运输数据集区分车辆类型（如轻型商用车、卡车、牵引车）、车辆大小及排放等级。铁路运输数据集按燃料分类（如电力和柴油）。内河、海洋、航空和管道运输按运输产品类型分类（如散货船或集装箱船）。所有运输方式还提供可运输冷藏产品的运输工具数据。

ecoinvent 数据库用于表示货物运输的功能单位是吨公里（metric ton\*km），表示将一吨货物运输一公里。运输数据按平均装载率计算，并纳入空载返程。

#### 旅客运输 <a href="#zh-b0013" id="zh-b0013"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库包含道路、铁路和航空客运数据。各运输方式均提供覆盖不同技术的数据集。具体而言，道路客运数据集涵盖不同车辆类型（如踏板摩托车、长途客车和有轨电车）。此外，数据库的汽车道路客运数据还区分车辆大小、燃料及排放等级。同时，铁路客运数据集按其运营区域类型区分（如城市、乡村、长途）。

对于旅客运输数据集，ecoinvent 数据库使用人公里（person\*km）单位，表示将一名旅客运输一公里；或使用公里（km）单位，表示将平均数量的旅客运输一公里。

#### 移动与固定基础设施 <a href="#zh-b0016" id="zh-b0016"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库提供运输工具生产、维护及寿命终结的数据，也提供相关固定基础设施（如道路、铁路轨道、港口、机场）建设、维护和退役的信息。

## Nowadays, transport contributes to 23% of global energy related GHG emissions. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

September 4th, 2025

In the ecoinvent database, the transport sector comprises over 600 datasets, covering the production, maintenance, and operation of transportation vehicles, the necessary infrastructure, and the end-of-life treatment. The sector also includes data for the modeling of refrigerated transport.

Nowadays, transport contributes to 23% of global energy-related GHG emissions. While transport emissions have more than doubled in the past fifty years, 80% of this increase is attributable to road transport. As reported in the [IPCC](https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2018/02/ipcc_wg3_ar5_chapter8.pdf) Climate Change Mitigation Assessment (2018), to reduce emissions from this sector, it is key to see its decoupling from economic growth rates.

### Sector Overview <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

The transport sector in the ecoinvent database comprises over 600 datasets, covering the production, maintenance, and operation of transportation vehicles, the necessary infrastructure, and the end-of-life treatment. The datasets included cover: road, rail, water, air, and pipeline transport. Each transport mode is split into different technologies based on relevant criteria such as vehicle size, fuel, type of transport goods, etc. Data on vehicles equipped for refrigerated transport are also available.

The geographies covered are Global (GLO), Europe (RER), and selected European countries, the United States (US), China (CN), South Africa (ZA), India (IN), and Brazil (BR).

### Sector Highlights <a href="#en-b0009" id="en-b0009"></a>

#### Freight Transport <a href="#en-b0010" id="en-b0010"></a>

The ecoinvent database includes datasets on freight transport, which may model freight transport by road, rail, inland waterways, sea, air as well as pipeline. Each of these modes  is represented by datasets on different technologies. Road transport is covered by datasets specific to vehicle type (e.g., light commercial vehicle, lorry, tractor), vehicle size, and emission class. Datasets on rail transport are categorized by fuel (e.g., electric and diesel). Inland waterways, sea, air, and pipeline transport are categorized based on the type of transport product (e.g., bulk, or container ship). Data on vehicles that allow transportation of refrigerated products are also available across all modes.

The functional unit used to represent freight transport in the ecoinvent database is tonne-kilometre (metric ton\*km), which represents the transport of one tonne of goods over one kilometre. Data on transport are calculated for an average load factor and include empty return trips.

#### Passenger Transport <a href="#en-b0013" id="en-b0013"></a>

The ecoinvent database includes data on passenger transport by road, rail, and air. For each of these modes, the database offers datasets covering different technologies. Specifically, road transport covered by datasets on different types of vehicles (e.g. scooter, coach, and tram). Even more, information on road transport by car in the database includes datasets to differentiate between size, fuel, and emission class of the vehicles. At the same time, datasets covering rail transport are distinguished by their geographical operation (e.g., urban, rural, long-distance).

For passenger transport datasets, the ecoinvent database uses either the person kilometer (person\*km) unit, representing the transport of one passenger over one kilometer, or the kilometer (km) unit, representing the transport of an average number of passengers over one kilometer.

#### Infrastructure Mobile and Immobile <a href="#en-b0016" id="en-b0016"></a>

The ecoinvent database offers data on vehicle production, maintenance, and end-of life. Information on the construction, maintenance, and decommissioning of relevant immobile infrastructure (e.g., road, railway track, port, airport) is also offered.
