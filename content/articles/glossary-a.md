### <strong>活动</strong> <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

ecoinvent 活动数据集表示某一人类活动的单元过程，以及该活动与环境及其他人类活动之间的交换。ecoinvent v3 数据库包含多种活动类型：转化活动、处理活动、市场活动、进出口活动、生产组合与供应组合等。

### <strong>活动类别</strong> <a href="#zh-b0009" id="zh-b0009"></a>

依据活动统计分类（例如国际标准产业分类 ISIC）中的某一类别归为一组的活动。

### <strong>活动链接（= 直接链接）</strong> <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

由数据集作者为某一特定交换指定、指向该交换的特定供应数据集的链接；它不同于数据库服务层在链接系统模型时自动添加的链接。

一般情况下，数据提供者不必提供活动链接，因为数据库服务层会自动添加这些链接。不过，如果某一组企业在供应链中联系十分紧密，且可以证明特定供应商的产量会随特定客户的需求而波动，则数据提供者应添加活动链接，以反映这一特定联系。

直接链接至特定供应活动的原因在已链接交换的注释字段中给出。当转化活动采用直接链接而绕过市场活动时，通常包含在市场活动中的活动和数据会直接添加到需要该投入的活动中。

### <strong>存量增加</strong> <a href="#zh-b0015" id="zh-b0015"></a>

寿命超过一年的副产品或废物。另见“基础设施”条目。

### <strong>AOX</strong> <a href="#zh-b0017" id="zh-b0017"></a>

可吸附有机卤素化合物（Adsorbable Organic Halogen Compounds）的缩写。

### <strong>Activity</strong> <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

An ecoinvent activity dataset represents a unit process of a human activity and its exchanges with the environment and with other human activities. There are several activity types in the ecoinvent v3 database; transforming activities, treatment activities, market activity, import and export activities, production and supply mixes, etc.

### <strong>Activity Class </strong> <a href="#en-b0009" id="en-b0009"></a>

Group of activities classified together under a heading in a statistical classification of activities, such as ISIC (International Standard Industrial Classification).

### <strong>Activity Link (= Direct Link)</strong> <a href="#en-b0011" id="en-b0011"></a>

A link for a specific exchange to a specific supplying dataset for that exchange as provided by the dataset author, as opposed to the links automatically added by the database service layer during linking of the system models.

In general, data providers do not need to supply activity links, since these are automatically added by the database service layer. However, if a specific group of enterprises are so closely linked in a supply chain that the production volumes of the specific suppliers can be shown to fluctuate with the demand of the specific customers, data providers shall add activity links to reflect this specific link.

The reason for linking directly to a specific supplying activity is provided in the comment field for the linked exchange. When transforming activities are linked directly, thus avoiding the market activities, the activities and data that are normally included with the market activities, are instead added directly to the activity requiring the input.

### <strong>Addition to Stock </strong> <a href="#en-b0015" id="en-b0015"></a>

By-product or waste with a lifetime in excess of one year. See also under infrastructure.

### <strong>AOX</strong> <a href="#en-b0017" id="en-b0017"></a>

Abbreviation for Adsorbable Organic Halogen Compounds
