2026年2月9日

将 ecoinvent 数据集成到软件和工具（包括内部工具）中，需要开发者许可证。

有关许可证方案的详细信息，请参阅[此处](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/)。

使用我们的[许可证指南](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/#license-calculator)，选择最符合您需求的许可证类型。

### <strong>如果我希望通过交互式工具向第三方提供 ecoinvent，需要考虑什么？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0029" id="zh-b0029"></a>

通过交互式工具向第三方提供 ecoinvent 数据，即使只在后台使用，您也成为数据库的“经销商”（reseller）。除单用户许可证之外，您还需要额外的再许可协议。年度再许可费用取决于所使用的数据集数量、影响指标数量和工具最终用户数量。

### <strong>直接向 ecoinvent 购买许可证与向软件供应商购买，价格是否不同？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0031" id="zh-b0031"></a>

您可以向我们购买[许可证](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/)，再将其集成到 LCA 软件工具中。某些 LCA 软件供应商收取集成费，请直接联系他们。

### ecoinvent 是否提供免费许可证？ <a href="#zh-b0033" id="zh-b0033"></a>

根据联合国经济和社会事务部（UN DESA）在[《2026 年世界经济形势与展望报告》](https://desapublications.un.org/file/21554/download)中发布的更新分类，低收入和中低收入国家的大学和学校可以获得免费许可证。建议教育机构的学生通过所在院系或教授负责人申请访问权限。

### 许可证升级 <a href="#zh-b0035" id="zh-b0035"></a>

许可证升级仅适用于永久许可证持有者。订阅许可证对应数据库当前最新版本。

### 如何确认许可证升级已成功处理，以及如何获取交易收据？ <a href="#zh-b0037" id="zh-b0037"></a>

通过在线信用卡支付，在 Accounts 平台完成许可证升级后，您将收到以下通知：

1. 您将收到 Stripe 的电子邮件，确认付款已成功处理。邮件还将说明如何访问或下载交易收据。
2. 您将收到 ecoinvent 的电子邮件，确认许可证已成功升级。邮件还将进一步说明升级包含的内容，以及如何在 ecoQuery 中访问 ecoinvent 数据库的最新版本。

### 许可证升级与维护延期有什么区别？ <a href="#zh-b0041" id="zh-b0041"></a>

许可证升级与维护延期没有区别；两者都指启用对自上一个许可证期间结束以来新发布的 ecoinvent 数据库版本的访问权限。通常，“维护延期”可能让人理解为延长许可证时间期限，但在此处，“许可证升级”特指更新许可证以允许访问最新数据库版本。两种表述均指这一更新过程，可以互换使用。

### <strong>为什么引入年度订阅模式？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0043" id="zh-b0043"></a>

此更新反映了我们的使用场景。年度订阅许可证在费用方面提供更高的灵活性和透明度。

  

<strong>年度订阅详情：</strong>

- 于 2025 年 4 月 1 日推出。

- 年度订阅许可证不会自动续订。

#### <strong>此更新会影响哪些用户？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0048" id="zh-b0048"></a>

此更新影响自 2025 年 4 月 1 日起的新客户。

此更新不影响现有客户，他们可以继续为永久许可证续订维护服务。

#### <strong>永久许可证与年度订阅许可证有什么区别？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0051" id="zh-b0051"></a>

<strong>永久许可证</strong>允许您一次性付款后无限期访问数据库。购买后，您拥有该许可证，可以无限期使用，无需额外费用；不过，更新或支持可能需要另行付费。

年度<strong>订阅许可证</strong>采用按年付费的订阅模式。许可证有效期为一年，您需要每年续订才能继续使用数据库。订阅包含持续更新、支持和新功能访问权限。

#### <strong>年度许可证能否访问所有更新和新功能？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0054" id="zh-b0054"></a>

年度许可证对应数据库当前最新版本。

#### <strong>如果取消年度许可证，会发生什么？我会失去访问权限吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0056" id="zh-b0056"></a>

如果您选择不续订，<strong>通过 ecoinvent 在线门户 ecoQuery 访问数据库的权限将在许可证到期日撤销</strong>。这意味着您将不能再获取或使用数据，即使您此前已下载副本。由于订阅模式不授予永久使用权，订阅到期后，继续使用任何已下载副本在法律上<strong>不被允许</strong>。要保持访问权限和继续使用已下载数据的合法权利，您必须维持有效订阅。

#### <strong>许可协议条款和条件有变化吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0058" id="zh-b0058"></a>

没有，许可协议的条款和条件没有变化。

### 年度订阅许可证的<strong>付款和开票</strong> <a href="#zh-b0060" id="zh-b0060"></a>

#### <strong>年度许可证如何收费？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0061" id="zh-b0061"></a>

您可以在[我们的网站](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/)选择凭发票付款或信用卡付款。

#### <strong>年度许可证会自动续订吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0063" id="zh-b0063"></a>

年度许可证不会自动续订。申请续订时，您必须在[此处](https://accounts.ecoinvent.org/)登录账户。

#### <strong>预付多年费用可以获得折扣吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0065" id="zh-b0065"></a>

我们不提供预付多年费用的选项。

#### <strong>一次购买多个许可证可以获得折扣吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0067" id="zh-b0067"></a>

我们不对多个单用户许可证或多用户教育许可证提供折扣。您可以将单用户许可证升级为[企业许可证](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/enterprise/)，最低套餐为五名用户。

### <strong>从永久许可证迁移至年度许可证（现有客户）</strong> <a href="#zh-b0069" id="zh-b0069"></a>

#### <strong>如果已购买永久许可证，可以改用年度许可证吗？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0070" id="zh-b0070"></a>

可以，您随时都能改用年度许可证。届时开始收取年度许可证费用，起始日期为购买时，许可证在一年后到期。

原有永久许可证不受影响，仍提供对原购买所含特定数据库版本的终身访问权限。永久许可证未覆盖的新版本发布、且您需要访问该较新版本时，改用年度许可证最为适用。

### <strong>其他说明</strong> <a href="#zh-b0073" id="zh-b0073"></a>

#### <strong>如果还有问题，或需要帮助理解新的许可模式，该怎么办？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0074" id="zh-b0074"></a>

如有其他问题，请使用我们的[联系表单](https://ecoinvent.org/contact-us/)。

### <strong>ecoinvent 的退款政策是什么？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0076" id="zh-b0076"></a>

付款成功后即无法退款。这是因为付款时许可证即被激活，用户可访问完整数据库，包括下载功能。

通过合作伙伴购买的 ecoinvent 许可证，相关商业问题应直接咨询该合作伙伴。

### 需要更多支持？ <a href="#zh-b0079" id="zh-b0079"></a>

有关登录问题、许可证、权利和限制、商业合作，或通过工具或应用使用 ecoinvent 的问题，请联系销售团队：[sales@ecoinvent.org](mailto:sales@ecoinvent.org)。

### <strong>What do I Have to Consider if I Want to Make ecoinvent Available for Third Parties Through an Interactive Tool?</strong> <a href="#en-b0029" id="en-b0029"></a>

When you make ecoinvent data available to third parties through an interactive tool (even in the background), you become a “reseller” of our database. You will need an additional sublicensing agreement on top of your single-user license. The yearly sublicensing fees depend on the number of datasets and impact indicators in use and the number of tool end users.

### <strong>Do Prices for ecoinvent Licenses Differ When Buying Directly From You vs. a Software Provider?</strong> <a href="#en-b0031" id="en-b0031"></a>

You can buy a [license](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/) from us and then integrate it into an LCA software tool. Some LCA providers charge integration fees, so please contact them directly.

### Does ecoinvent Offer Free Licenses? <a href="#en-b0033" id="en-b0033"></a>

Free-of-charge licenses are available for universities and schools in low- and low-middle-income countries according to the updated classification from the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs (UN DESA), published in the [World Economic Situation and Prospects Report 2026](https://desapublications.un.org/file/21554/download). Students at educational institutions are recommended to apply for access through their faculty or professorship lead.

### License Upgrades <a href="#en-b0035" id="en-b0035"></a>

License upgrades are only applicable to perpetual license holders. Subscription licenses are attached to the current, latest version of the database.

### How Do I Ensure that My License Upgrade Has Been Processed Successfully, and How Can I Obtain a Receipt for the Transaction? <a href="#en-b0037" id="en-b0037"></a>

Once you have completed the process of upgrading your license on the Accounts platform through an online credit card payment, you will receive the following communications:

1. You will receive an email from Stripe confirming that your payment has been successfully processed. This email will also contain directions on how to access or download a receipt for the transaction.
2. You will receive an email from ecoinvent confirming that your license has been successfully upgraded. This email will also contain further information on what is included in your license upgrade and provide directions on how to access the latest version of the ecoinvent database on ecoQuery.

### What Is The Difference Between a License Upgrade and a Maintenance Extension? <a href="#en-b0041" id="en-b0041"></a>

There is no difference between a license upgrade and a maintenance extension; both terms refer to the process of enabling access to newer versions of the ecoinvent database that have been released since your last license period ended. Traditionally, a maintenance extension might suggest extending the time duration of a license, but here, a license upgrade specifically means an update to your license to allow access to the latest database versions. The terms are used synonymously to refer to this update process.

### <strong>Why Introduce an Annual Subscription Model?</strong> <a href="#en-b0043" id="en-b0043"></a>

This update reflects our use cases. An annual subscription license offers more flexibility and transparency regarding fees.

  

<strong>Details about the annual subscription: </strong>

- It was released on April 1st, 2025.

- The annual subscription license does not automatically renew.

#### <strong>Who Will Be Affected by This Update?</strong> <a href="#en-b0048" id="en-b0048"></a>

This update affects new customers starting April 1st, 2025.

This update does not affect our existing customers, who can continue to renew their maintenance for perpetual licenses.

#### <strong>What Is the Difference Between a Perpetual License and an Annual Subscription License?</strong> <a href="#en-b0051" id="en-b0051"></a>

A <strong>perpetual license</strong> allows you to make a one-time payment for indefinite access to the database. Once purchased, you own the license and can use it indefinitely without any additional fees, though you may need to pay for updates or support separately.

An annual <strong>subscription license</strong>, on the other hand, is a subscription model where you pay on an annual basis. The license is valid for one year, and you will need to renew it each year to continue using the database. This model includes ongoing updates, support, and access to new features as part of the subscription.

#### <strong>Will I Have Access to All Updates and New Features With an Annual License?</strong> <a href="#en-b0054" id="en-b0054"></a>

The annual licenses are attached to the current latest version of the database.

#### <strong>What Happens if I Cancel My Annual License? Will I Lose Access?</strong> <a href="#en-b0056" id="en-b0056"></a>

If you choose not to renew your subscription, <strong>your access to the database through ecoQuery, ecoinvent’s online portal, will be revoked at the license expiry date</strong>. This means you will no longer be able to retrieve or use the data, even if you previously downloaded a copy. Since the subscription model does not grant perpetual usage rights, you are <strong>not legally permitted</strong> to continue using any downloaded copies after your subscription expires. To maintain access and the legal right to continue working with any downloaded data, you must keep an active subscription.

#### <strong>Are There Any Changes to the Terms and Conditions of the License Agreement?</strong> <a href="#en-b0058" id="en-b0058"></a>

No, the terms and conditions of the license agreement have not changed.

### <strong>Payment and Billing</strong> of the Annual Subscription License <a href="#en-b0060" id="en-b0060"></a>

#### <strong>How Will I Be Billed for the Annual License?</strong> <a href="#en-b0061" id="en-b0061"></a>

You can choose to pay by invoice or by credit card from [our website](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/).

#### <strong>Will the Annual License Automatically Renew?</strong> <a href="#en-b0063" id="en-b0063"></a>

The annual license will not automatically renew. To request a renewal, you must login to your account [here](https://accounts.ecoinvent.org/).

#### <strong>Will I Receive a Discount for Paying Upfront for Multiple Years?</strong> <a href="#en-b0065" id="en-b0065"></a>

We do not offer the option to pay upfront for multiple years.

#### <strong>Will I Receive a Discount for Purchasing Multiple Licenses Upfront?</strong> <a href="#en-b0067" id="en-b0067"></a>

We do not offer discounts for multiple single-user licenses or multi-user educational licenses. We do offer the option to upgrade your single-user license to an [Enterprise license](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/enterprise/), starting with a package of five users.

### <strong>Migration from Perpetual to Annual Licenses (for existing customers)</strong> <a href="#en-b0069" id="en-b0069"></a>

#### <strong>If I Purchased a Perpetual License, Can I Switch to an Annual License?</strong> <a href="#en-b0070" id="en-b0070"></a>

Yes, switching to an annual license is possible at any time. This will activate the annual license fee, with the start date set to the time of purchase and the license expiring one year later.

The original perpetual license remains unaffected, providing lifelong access to the specific database version included in that purchase. Switching to an annual license is most relevant when a new release becomes available that is not covered under the perpetual license, and access to this more recent version is required.
