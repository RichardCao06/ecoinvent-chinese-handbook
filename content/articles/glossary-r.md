### 世界其他地区（RoW） <a href="#zh-b0004" id="zh-b0004"></a>

这是一个动态概念：当同一活动、时间段和宏观经济情景同时存在全球数据集及一个或多个非全球数据集时，便会出现这一概念。其定义针对每项活动而定，取决于该特定活动名称有哪些已定义的地理区域。地理位置为世界其他地区的活动由数据库服务层在链接过程中自动生成。在 ecoinvent v3.2（2015 年）及更高版本中，RoW 数据集通过完全复制 GLO 数据集并调整不确定性而生成。随后，新生成的 RoW 数据集与适当地区的活动链接，从而建立 RoW 特有的供应链。

### RoW <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

世界其他地区（rest of the world）的缩写。

### Rest of the World (RoW) <a href="#en-b0004" id="en-b0004"></a>

A dynamic concept that exists in the situation when both a global dataset and one or more non-global datasets are available for the same activity, time period, and macro-economic scenario. The definition is specific to each activity and depends on what defined geographies are available for the specific activity name. The activities with the Rest-of-the-World geographic location are generated automatically during the linking by the database service layer. In ecoinvent v3.2 (2015) and higher the RoW is generated as an exact copy of the GLO dataset with uncertainty adjusted. The newly generated RoW is then linked with activities of an adequate geographies creating RoW specific supply chain.

### RoW <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

Abbreviation for rest of the world
