## ecoinvent 数据库每年更新，扩展部门和地理覆盖范围。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2023 年 11 月 8 日

ecoinvent 数据库始于 20 世纪 90 年代末的一个项目，旨在建立一致、透明的生命周期清单（LCI）数据库，用于基于生命周期的评价。在过去二十年间，该项目发展为 ecoinvent 协会，成为 ecoinvent 数据库的发布者，以及在环境评价数据管理领域受到全球信赖的合作伙伴。

2003 年，ecoinvent 项目促成了 ecoinvent 数据库第一个版本的发布。此后，项目合作方继续在 ecoinvent 中心（瑞士生命周期清单中心）框架下开展合作。ecoinvent 第 1 版发布后，数据库的全面修订、更新和扩展工作随即启动，最终于 2007 年发布 ecoinvent 第 2 版。

ecoinvent 数据库第 3 版于 2013 年发布。此后，数据库每年更新，发布新增和更新数据，扩展数据库的部门及地理覆盖范围。每个版本均建立在此前所有版本的基础上。

### 第 3 版的承诺 <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

#### 改进供应链表示 <a href="#zh-b0008" id="zh-b0008"></a>

与每次版本发布一样，数据库中现有供应链经过全面审阅，以反映全球和区域产品流的当前实际情况。因此，需求活动与供应活动之间的链接已更新，并引入新的区域市场数据集。

#### 属性 <a href="#zh-b0010" id="zh-b0010"></a>

ecoinvent 第 3 版中所有具有质量的产品和基本流交换都至少具备六项属性：干质量、湿质量、湿质量中的水质量、含水率、化石碳含量和非化石碳含量。此外，数据库中的每种产品均有价格，可用于经济分配等用途。

#### 系统模型 <a href="#zh-b0012" id="zh-b0012"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库是唯一支持三种[系统模型](system-models.md)的主要 LCI 数据库：按分类截断的分配模型（Allocation cut-off by classification）、替代点分配模型（Allocation at the Point of Substitution）以及后果型模型（Consequential）。此外，对于希望访问[未链接单元过程](../assets/8ce9820c1aeb-activity_overview_3.7.1_undefined_public.xlsx)的所有第 3 版用户，ecoinvent 在其网站上提供未指定系统模型版本。

## The ecoinvent database is updated yearly with expanded sectorial and geographical coverage. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Nov 8th, 2023

The ecoinvent database started off in the late 1990s as a project aiming to develop a consistent, transparent database for Life Cycle Inventory (LCI) data to be used in life cycle–based assessments. During the past twenty years, the project has developed into the ecoinvent association, the publisher of the ecoinvent database and a worldwide, trusted partner in the management of data for environmental assessments.

In 2003 the ecoinvent project resulted in the release of the first version of the ecoinvent database. After this launch, the project partners continued their cooperation under the ecoinvent Centre, the Swiss Centre for Life Cycle Inventories. Following the launch of ecoinvent version 1, the work on a complete revision, update and extension of the database began, which resulted in the release of ecoinvent version 2 in 2007.

Version 3 of the ecoinvent database was released in 2013. Since then, the database is updated yearly. New and updated data are released, expanding the coverage of the database sectorially and geographically. Each version builds on all previous versions.

### Version 3 Commitments <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

#### Improved Supply Chain Representation <a href="#en-b0008" id="en-b0008"></a>

As with every release, the existing supply chains in the database were thoroughly reviewed in order to reflect the current reality of global and regional product flows. As a result, links between demanding and supplying activities have been updated, and new, regional market datasets have been introduced.”

#### Properties <a href="#en-b0010" id="en-b0010"></a>

All products and elementary exchanges with mass in ecoinvent version 3 come with at least six properties; dry mass, wet mass, water in wet mass, water content, carbon content fossil and non fossil. Additionally, every single product in the database has a price that can be used among other things for economic allocation.”

#### System Models <a href="#en-b0012" id="en-b0012"></a>

The ecoinvent database is the only major LCI database supporting three [system models](system-models.md): Allocation cut-off by classification, Allocation at the Point of Substitution, and Consequential. In addition, for all version 3 users preferring to have access to [unlinked unit processes](../assets/8ce9820c1aeb-activity_overview_3.7.1_undefined_public.xlsx), ecoinvent offers an undefined version on its website.
