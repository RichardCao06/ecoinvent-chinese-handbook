## 水在人类社会和自然生态系统中均发挥关键作用。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2023 年 9 月 30 日

超过 20 亿人生活在高度水资源压力国家（[联合国，2018](https://www.unwater.org/app/uploads/2018/12/SDG6_SynthesisReport2018_WaterandSanitation_04122018.pdf)），这些国家可用的可再生淡水资源有相当大部分被取用。因此，管理水资源以满足不同使用者需求、同时尊重周边环境要求，变得日益重要。

了解商品和服务生产期间的取水量、耗水量和排水量，可为水资源管理提供充分依据，并估算用水对环境的潜在影响。

### <strong>部</strong><strong>门概况</strong> <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库的供水部门包含 150 多个数据集，对水的提取、处理和分配建模。数据库提供三种主要水类型的数据：

- 自来水
- 工艺用水（主要用于工业过程）
- 灌溉用水

水生产数据集覆盖欧洲、亚洲、非洲以及南北美洲的二十个地区和国家。

### <strong>部</strong><strong>门亮点</strong> <a href="#zh-b0012" id="zh-b0012"></a>

#### 自来水 <a href="#zh-b0013" id="zh-b0013"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库中的自来水指由集中式市政供水系统处理并供应的水。自来水可用于家庭，也可用于商业或工业用途。数据库将自来水生产与供应分为两个步骤：“tap water production”活动对水的提取和处理建模，自来水市场则对向最终使用者分配自来水建模。

数据库对欧洲、印度、巴西、秘鲁、哥伦比亚和南非等多个地区和国家的自来水生产与分配建模。所提取水的来源以及各国不同的水质要求决定所需处理技术。ecoinvent 数据库提供对以下处理类型建模的数据集：

- 常规处理（混凝、絮凝和沉淀）
- 地下水的化学处理或消毒
- 直接过滤
- 微滤筛处理
- 超滤
- 采用常规或超滤预处理的海水反渗透

自来水市场活动纳入分配水所需的基础设施数量，并考虑处理和分配期间发生的损失。

#### 工艺用水 <a href="#zh-b0023" id="zh-b0023"></a>

某些生产过程需要满足特定水质标准的水。ecoinvent 数据库提供四种可作为工业过程输入的水的生产建模数据（Gmünder et al., 2019）：

1. 完全软化水：指经过处理，去除了可能沉淀并积聚在设备及管道中的矿物质的水。
2. 脱碳水：指去除了有机悬浮物和碳酸氢钙的水，例如用于冷却塔和烟气洗涤器。
3. 去离子水：指几乎去除了全部矿物离子（如铁、钠、钙、铜、硫酸根和氯离子）的水，通常用于冷却系统、机械润滑以及化妆品和药品生产。
4. 超纯水：指为满足极严格的质量规范而经过深度净化的水，主要用于半导体行业、发电和药品生产。

#### <strong>灌溉</strong> <a href="#zh-b0029" id="zh-b0029"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库提供三种不同灌溉技术的数据集：

- 地面灌溉：仅利用重力供水，可淹灌整个田块，或将水引入小渠道或条状土地。
- 喷灌：将水喷入空中，以模拟自然降雨。
- 滴灌：通过管道系统将水直接施加到植物附近的土壤上，使根系生长的土壤仅有部分被润湿。

对于喷灌和滴灌，每 m3 取水的能源消耗量考虑灌溉技术，包括泵效率和平均提水深度等因素。还考虑水源（地表水、地下水或其他来源），其比例因国家而异。

ecoinvent 数据库包含对巴西、法国、印度、秘鲁、南非和美国等十五个以上国家灌溉建模的数据集。

## Water plays a vital role both in human societies and natural ecosystems. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Sept 30th, 2023

With over 2 billion people living in countries experiencing high water stress ([United Nations, 2018](https://www.unwater.org/app/uploads/2018/12/SDG6_SynthesisReport2018_WaterandSanitation_04122018.pdf)), whereby a significant fraction of the available renewable freshwater resources is withdrawn, it is becoming more and more crucial to manage water resources such that the needs of different consumers are met, while respecting the requirements of the surrounding environment.

Knowing how much water is extracted, consumed, and discharged during the production of goods and services allows to make well-informed decisions concerning the management of water resources, as well as estimate the potential impacts of water use on the environment.

### <strong>S</strong><strong>ector Overview</strong> <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

The water supply sector in the ecoinvent database comprises over 150 datasets which model the extraction, treatment, and distribution of water. Data for three main types of water are available in the ecoinvent database:

- Tap water
- Process water (used mainly for industrial processes)
- Irrigation water

The geographies of the water production datasets cover twenty regions and countries located on the continents of Europe, Asia, Africa, and North and South America.

### <strong>S</strong><strong>ector Highlights</strong> <a href="#en-b0012" id="en-b0012"></a>

#### Tap Water <a href="#en-b0013" id="en-b0013"></a>

In the ecoinvent database, tap water represents water that is treated and supplied by centralized municipal water supply systems. The tap water may be consumed in households or can be used for commercial or industrial purposes. The production and supply of tap water consists of two steps in the ecoinvent database: the extraction and treatment of water is modeled in the “tap water production” activities, while the markets for tap water model the distribution of tap water to the end-consumer.

The production and distribution of tap water is modeled for several regions and countries, including Europe, India, Brazil, Peru, Colombia, and South Africa. Depending on the origin of the water that is extracted and the quality requirements, which vary between countries, a different treatment technology is required. Datasets that model the following types of treatments are available in the ecoinvent database:

- conventional (coagulation, flocculation and sedimentation)
- underground water with chemical treatment or disinfection
- direct filtration
- microstrainer
- ultrafiltration
- seawater reverse osmosis with conventional or ultrafiltration pretreatment

The market activities for tap water include the amount of infrastructure needed to distribute the water and take into account losses that occur during the treatment and distribution.

#### Process Water <a href="#en-b0023" id="en-b0023"></a>

For certain production processes, water that meets specific quality criteria is required. The ecoinvent database provides data modeling the production of four types of water that may be used as input to industrial processes (Gmünder et al., 2019):

1. Completely softened water: corresponds to water that has undergone treatment to remove minerals that may precipitate and accumulate in equipment and piping.
2. Decarbonized water: represents water from which organic suspended matter and calcium bicarbonate have been removed. It is used for example in water cooling towers and flue gas scrubbers.
3. Deionised water: is water that has had almost all its mineral ions removed (such as iron, sodium, calcium, copper, sulphate, and chloride). It is typically used in cooling systems, machine lubrication, and the production of cosmetics and medicine.
4. Ultrapure water: represents water that has been heavily purified in order to meet very strict quality specifications. It is mostly used in the semiconductor industry, for power generation and for the production of pharmaceuticals.

#### <strong>Irrigation</strong> <a href="#en-b0029" id="en-b0029"></a>

Datasets for three different irrigation technologies are available in the ecoinvent database:

- Surface irrigation: the water is applied simply using gravity, either by flooding the entire field, or by feeding the water into small channels or strips of land.
- Sprinkler irrigation: the water is sprayed into the air, in order to mimic natural rainfall.
- Drip irrigation: the water is applied from a system of pipes directly onto the soil, close to the plants so that only part of the soil in which the roots grow is wetted

For sprinkler and drip irrigation, the amount of energy consumed per m3 of withdrawn takes into account the irrigation technology, which considers, among other things, the pump efficiency and the average depth from which water is pumped. The origin of the water is also considered (surface, ground or other sources), for which the shares vary from one country to another.

The ecoinvent database contains datasets that model irrigation in over fifteen countries, such as Brazil, France, India, Peru, South Africa, and the United States of America.
