### 咸水 <a href="#zh-b0009" id="zh-b0009"></a>

盐浓度较高的水。

### 服务 <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

没有质量的产品。

### 浅层地下水 <a href="#zh-b0013" id="zh-b0013"></a>

位于地下不足三米深处的地下水。

### SPOLD <a href="#zh-b0015" id="zh-b0015"></a>

生命周期发展促进协会（Society for the Promotion of Lifecycle Development）的缩写。

### 供应组合 <a href="#zh-b0017" id="zh-b0017"></a>

在生产组合的基础上，加上指定产品进入该地理区域的进口。

### 地表水 <a href="#zh-b0019" id="zh-b0019"></a>

可见的地表径流、河流和湖泊中的淡水，不包括海水。不区分湖水与河水。

### 系统模型 <a href="#zh-b0021" id="zh-b0021"></a>

描述活动数据集如何链接以构成产品系统的模型。系统模型可决定某些因素，例如是否采用分配（以及采用哪一种分配）或替代来处理多功能性问题，或者采用平均供应商还是边际供应商。它也可能影响副产品处理的评价方式。

同义词（投入产出经济学）：技术模型。

### Salt Water <a href="#en-b0009" id="en-b0009"></a>

Water having high concentration of salts.

### Service <a href="#en-b0011" id="en-b0011"></a>

A product without a mass.

### Shallow Groundwater <a href="#en-b0013" id="en-b0013"></a>

Groundwater that is located less than three meters deep.

### SPOLD <a href="#en-b0015" id="en-b0015"></a>

Abbreviation for Society for the Promotion of Lifecycle Development

### Supply Mix <a href="#en-b0017" id="en-b0017"></a>

A production mix with the addition of the import of the specified product to the geographical area.

### Surface Water <a href="#en-b0019" id="en-b0019"></a>

Fresh water you can see as overland flow, rivers, and lakes excluding sea water. A distinction is not made between lake and river water.

### System Model <a href="#en-b0021" id="en-b0021"></a>

A model describing how activity datasets are linked to form product systems. A system model may determine factors such as whether to use allocation (and which type of allocation) or substitution to handle the multi-functionality problem, or whether to use average or marginal suppliers. It may also affect how by-product treatments are assessed.

Synonym (input-output economics): Technology model.
