## 了解正确设置 ecoinvent 数据库访问权限的最初步骤。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2025 年 5 月 12 日

### <strong>如何访问 ecoinvent 数据库？</strong> <a href="#zh-b0004" id="zh-b0004"></a>

拥有许可证后，可通过我们的平台[ecoQuery](https://db.ecoinvent.org/)或主流软件工具访问 ecoinvent 数据库。

了解我们的[软件合作伙伴](https://ecoinvent.org/partners/?category=software-tools)。

### <strong>如何购买 ecoinvent 许可证</strong> <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

购买 ecoinvent 许可证的第一步是了解我们提供的许可方案。我们的[许可证指南](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/#license-calculator)可帮助您确定最适合您的用途和实际情况的许可证。

有关定价和购买选项的更多信息，请参阅我们的[许可证页面](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/)。

### <strong>首次登录</strong> <a href="#zh-b0010" id="zh-b0010"></a>

购买 ecoinvent 许可证并首次登录 ecoinvent 数据库后，系统会要求您更新个人资料。

请通过添加大写字母、数字或特殊字符选择一个安全的密码，以提高账户安全性。

如果您从我们的软件合作伙伴处获取登录凭据，您的 LCA 软件提供商（例如 PRé、ifu、GreenDelta）很可能为您提供了默认用户名。请将用户名个性化。

## Discover the first steps to correctly set up your access to the ecoinvent database. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

May 12th, 2025

### <strong>How Can I Access the ecoinvent Database?</strong> <a href="#en-b0004" id="en-b0004"></a>

With a license, the ecoinvent database can be accessed on our platform, [ecoQuery](https://db.ecoinvent.org/), or through leading software tools.

Discover our [software partners](https://ecoinvent.org/partners/?category=software-tools).

### <strong>How to Purchase an ecoinvent License</strong> <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

The first step to purchasing an ecoinvent license is to understand our available offerings. Our [license guide](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/#license-calculator) can help you determine the best license for your purpose and situation.

More information about our pricing and purchasing options is available on our [licenses page](https://ecoinvent.org/licenses/).

### <strong>Logging in for the First Time</strong> <a href="#en-b0010" id="en-b0010"></a>

After purchasing an ecoinvent license and logging in to the ecoinvent database for the first time, you will be asked to update your profile.

Make sure that you choose a safe password by adding capital letters, numbers, or special characters to improve the security of your account.

If you received your login credentials from one of our software partners, you were likely given a default username by your LCA software provider (e.g., PRé, ifu, GreenDelta). Please personalize your username.
