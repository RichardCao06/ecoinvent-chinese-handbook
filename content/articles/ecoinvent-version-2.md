## ecoinvent 第 2 版现已在 ecoQuery 中提供。第 2 版于 2007 年首次发布，很快成为全球最广为认可的 LCI 数据库之一。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2024年6月1日

### 2024 年，ecoinvent 数据库第 2 版从外部平台迁移至 ecoQuery。 <a href="#zh-b0004" id="zh-b0004"></a>

#### 如何访问 v2： <a href="#zh-b0005" id="zh-b0005"></a>

1. 登录 ecoQuery。首次登录 ecoQuery 的说明见[此处](first-log-in-to-ecoinvent.md)。
2. 进入页面顶部的“文件”（Files）选项卡。
3. 向下滚动到“2.”部分。

如需更多 ecoQuery 使用支持，请参阅[入门指南](https://support.ecoinvent.org/getting-started)。

如有关于 v2 迁移至 ecoQuery 的问题，请[联系我们](https://support.ecoinvent.org/kb-tickets/new)。

### 有意升级许可证？ <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

#### 获取 ecoinvent 数据库[最新版本](../topics/version-3.md)的访问权限。 <a href="#zh-b0012" id="zh-b0012"></a>

数据库每年更新，扩展部门和地理覆盖范围。您可以[登录账户](https://accounts.ecoinvent.org/)，在线升级许可证。

## ecoinvent version 2 is now available on ecoQuery. First released in 2007, version 2 quickly became one of the most widely recognized LCI databases worldwide. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

June 1st, 2024

### In 2024, version 2 of the ecoinvent database was moved from an external platform to ecoQuery. <a href="#en-b0004" id="en-b0004"></a>

#### How to access v2: <a href="#en-b0005" id="en-b0005"></a>

1. Login to ecoQuery. Instructions about how to log in to ecoQuery for the first time are available [here](first-log-in-to-ecoinvent.md).
2. Navigate to the "Files" tab at the top of the page.
3. Scroll down to section "2."

For additional support with using ecoQuery, please visit our [Getting Started](https://support.ecoinvent.org/getting-started) guide.

Please [contact us](https://support.ecoinvent.org/kb-tickets/new) if you have any questions regarding the transition of v2 to ecoQuery.

### Interested in upgrading your license? <a href="#en-b0011" id="en-b0011"></a>

#### Get access to the [latest version](../topics/version-3.md) of the ecoinvent database. <a href="#en-b0012" id="en-b0012"></a>

Our database is updated yearly with expanded sectorial and geographical coverage. You can upgrade your license online by [logging into your account](https://accounts.ecoinvent.org/).
