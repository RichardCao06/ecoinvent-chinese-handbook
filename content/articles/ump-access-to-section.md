## 如何获取本部分文章的访问权限。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

本类别的文章介绍通过门户提供的用户管理功能。<strong>这些文章仅供门户管理员访问。</strong>

如果您是门户管理员，但无法看到本部分的任何文章，则可能需要使用管理员账户所用的同一电子邮件地址[<strong>登录或注册账户</strong>](https://support.ecoinvent.org/_hcms/mem/login)。

如果登录后仍无法访问这些文章，或者希望申请管理员访问权限，请联系我们获取支持。

- 经销商请通过此邮箱联系我们：[developer.pro@ecoinvent.org](mailto:developer.pro@ecoinvent.org)
- 企业客户请联系您的客户经理，或通过此邮箱联系：[support@ecoinvent.org](mailto:support@ecoinvent.org)

## How to get access to articles in this section. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Articles in this category cover user management functions available through the portals. <strong>These articles are only available to portal admins. </strong>

If you are a portal admin and you can't see any articles in this section, you might need to [<strong>log in or register for an account</strong>](https://support.ecoinvent.org/_hcms/mem/login) with the same email address used for your admin account.

If you still can't access the articles after logging in, or if you want to request admin access, please contact us for support.

- For resellers, contact us at [developer.pro@ecoinvent.org](mailto:developer.pro@ecoinvent.org)
- For enterprise customers, contact your account manager or [support@ecoinvent.org](mailto:support@ecoinvent.org)
