## 旅游业是许多经济体的重要部门，也是一个快速增长的行业，涵盖住宿服务等多个方面。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2024 年 1 月 30 日

### 部门概况 <a href="#zh-b0004" id="zh-b0004"></a>

ecoinvent 数据库的住宿服务部门包含五十个数据集，涵盖不同舒适档次旅游住宿设施的建设和运营，以及所需的消费品，包括家具和电器。所覆盖的地理区域为巴西（BR）和秘鲁（PE）。

### 部门亮点 <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

#### 住宿设施建设 <a href="#zh-b0007" id="zh-b0007"></a>

数据库包含旅舍、经济型酒店、高档酒店和豪华酒店等不同舒适档次住宿设施的建设数据。这些数据集纳入了设施建设所用的材料输入（如混凝土、胶合板、门、窗），以及维护和退役所需的材料。为恰当体现设施维护，数据库中的住宿设施建设数据集根据材料寿命相对于建筑生命周期的长短，调整所纳入的材料用量；建筑生命周期假定为七十五年。同时，数据集将废弃材料作为副产品纳入，以对退役进行建模。  
  
有关输入材料的所有信息均根据酒店调查以及建筑师对设计方案和图纸的审阅汇编，并辅以二手数据。

#### 住宿设施运营 <a href="#zh-b0009" id="zh-b0009"></a>

数据库包含旅舍、经济型酒店、高档酒店和豪华酒店等不同舒适档次住宿设施的运营数据。所有数据集均采用一个“客夜”（guest night，即每人住宿一晚）作为功能单位。这是使用最广泛的功能单位，可用于比较规模和舒适档次不同的住宿服务。入住率来源于所开展的住宿服务调查。  
  
住宿设施运营数据集纳入建筑建设、消费品以及公用服务的输入。消费品及其定期翻新投入根据其使用寿命相对于住宿设施运营寿命的长短进行调整。

#### 消费品 <a href="#zh-b0011" id="zh-b0011"></a>

数据库包含不同类型客房及公共空间中配备或使用的物品信息，如微波炉、洗衣机、家具和床垫。每种物品均由生产和运营数据集表示，并通过将废弃材料作为副产品纳入，计及这些物品的处置。

所需消费品的详细信息通过酒店调查收集，并辅以二手数据。

## A key sector for many economies, Tourism is a rapidly growing industry including many aspects, such as accommodation services. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

January 30th 2024

### Sector Overview <a href="#en-b0004" id="en-b0004"></a>

The Accommodation Services sector in the ecoinvent database comprises fifty datasets, covering the construction and operation of various comfort categories of tourist accommodation facilities, as well as the necessary consumer goods, including furniture and appliances. The geographies covered are Brazil (BR) and Peru (PE).

### Sector Highlights <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

#### Accommodation Construction <a href="#en-b0007" id="en-b0007"></a>

The database contains data on the construction of various comfort categories, including hostel, budget, upmarket, and luxury hotel. These datasets integrate both the material inputs (e.g., concrete, plywood, door, window) used for building the facilities, as well as for maintaining and decommissioning them. To properly represent the maintenance of such facilities, the accommodation construction datasets in the database integrate the amounts of materials used, which are adjusted based on the lifetime of the materials compared with the building’s life cycle, which is assumed to be seventy-five years. At the same time, the datasets integrate the waste materials as by-products in order to model decommissioning.   
  
All information regarding input materials has been compiled based on hotel surveys, review of plans and blueprints by architects, and has been complemented with secondary data.

#### Accommodation Operation <a href="#en-b0009" id="en-b0009"></a>

The database contains data on the operation of various comfort categories, including hostel, budget, upmarket, and luxury hotel. All datasets use the functional unit of one “guest night” (one night per person). This is the most widely used functional unit providing a way to compare different sizes and comfort categories of accommodation services. The occupancy rate is sourced from the conducted accommodation services surveys.   
  
Accommodation operation datasets integrate the input of the building construction, consumer goods as well as utilities. The inputs of consumer goods and their regular refurbishments are adjusted based on their lifetime compared with the operation lifetime of the accommodation facility.

#### Consumer Goods <a href="#en-b0011" id="en-b0011"></a>

The database includes information on items present or utilized in various types of rooms as well as common spaces, such as microwave ovens, washing machines, furniture, and mattresses. Each item is represented by production and operation datasets, accounting for the disposal of the items by incorporating waste materials as by-products.

Details regarding the required consumer goods have been gathered through hotel surveys and supplemented with secondary data.
