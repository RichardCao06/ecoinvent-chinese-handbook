## ecoinvent 数据库以 ecoSpold 格式提供。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

可将数据文件的 .spold 扩展名改为 .xml，从而在任意 XML 编辑器中查看任何活动的全部[单元过程原始数据（UPR）](upr-lci-lcia.md)、[累积生命周期清单（LCI）](upr-lci-lcia.md)以及[生命周期影响评价（LCIA）](impact-assessment.md)结果。

为便于阅读，ecoinvent 数据汇总于不同文件中。ecoinvent 数据库第 3 版采用 ecoSpold2 格式，第 1 版和第 2 版采用 ecoSpold 1 格式。

此外，所有系统模型下全部数据集的累积 LCI，以及整个数据库的 LCIA 结果，均可通过 Excel 格式获取。这些 .xlsx 文件位于 [ecoQuery](https://ecoquery.ecoinvent.org/3.9.1/cutoff/search) 的文件栏目中。

## The ecoinvent database is available in the ecoSpold format. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

The data type .spold can be overwritten to .xml and thus all the [unit processes (UPR)](upr-lci-lcia.md), [cumulative life cycle inventory (LCI),](upr-lci-lcia.md) and results of the [impact assessment (LCIA)](impact-assessment.md) of any activity can be viewed in any xml editor.

The ecoinvent data is summarized in different files to ease readability. Version 3 of the ecoinvent database relied on ecoSpold2 format. Version 1 and 2 of the ecoinvent database relied on ecoSpold 1 format.

In addition, cumulative LCI of all datasets as well as LCIA results for the entire database are accessible for all system models in excel format. These .xlsx files are available in the files section of [ecoQuery](https://ecoquery.ecoinvent.org/3.9.1/cutoff/search).
