## 适用于 ecoinvent 第 3 版的免费软件 ecoEditor，是用于为 ecoinvent 数据库未来版本创建、编辑、审阅和上传数据集的工具。 <a href="#zh-b0002" id="zh-b0002"></a>

2023 年 9 月 25 日

ecoEditor 取代了旧版 ecoEditor 1（仅供内部使用）及基于电子表格的 ecoSpoldAccess。ecoEditor 可免费使用。

### <strong>ecoEditor 软件</strong> <a href="#zh-b0005" id="zh-b0005"></a>

#### <strong>供数据提供方使用的免费软件 ecoEditor</strong> <a href="#zh-b0006" id="zh-b0006"></a>

“ecoEditor for ecoinvent version 3”软件可免费使用。

ecoEditor 主要用于向 ecoinvent 提交数据，并非用于访问 ecoinvent 数据库。如果您仅希望访问数据库、查看或下载数据集，请通过 ecoQuery [登录数据库](https://ecoinvent.org/database-login/)。

介绍该软件的简要文档会作为 ecoEditor 的组成部分安装到您的计算机上。此外，ecoEditor 的四部分视听教程也对该工具作了基本介绍。

如需更多详细信息，请[联系我们](https://ecoinvent.org/contact-us/)。

#### 访问未链接的数据集 <a href="#zh-b0014" id="zh-b0014"></a>

ecoEditor 让您能够访问 ecoinvent 的生产数据库；换言之，您能够访问 ecoinvent 数据库未来版本中所有未链接数据集的最新已接受状态。借助 ecoEditor 工具，您可以下载这些数据集（例如，为准备这些数据的修订版本，或以已有数据集为基础创建新数据集）。

下载需要数据提供方账户，可直接通过软件申请；下载数量限制为每天十个数据集。

希望您享受使用 ecoEditor 的过程，我们期待您为数据库的改进和扩展作出贡献。

## The freeware ecoEditor for ecoinvent version 3 is the tool to create, edit, review, and upload datasets for the future versions of the ecoinvent database. <a href="#en-b0002" id="en-b0002"></a>

Sept 25th, 2023

ecoEditor replaces the old (only internally used) ecoEditor 1 and the spreadsheet-based ecoSpoldAccess. The ecoEditor is free to use.

### <strong>The ecoEditor Software</strong> <a href="#en-b0005" id="en-b0005"></a>

#### <strong>Freeware ecoEditor for Data Provider</strong> <a href="#en-b0006" id="en-b0006"></a>

The software “ecoEditor for ecoinvent version 3” is free to use.

The primary use of the ecoEditor is to submit data to ecoinvent and not for accessing the ecoinvent database. If you would simply like to access the database, view or download datasets, please [login to the database](https://ecoinvent.org/database-login/) through ecoQuery.

A document providing a short introduction to the software is installed on your computer as part of the ecoEditor. In addition, a four part audio-visual tutorials of ecoEditor offer a basic introduction to the tool.

For more information detailed information please [contact us](https://ecoinvent.org/contact-us/).

#### Access to Unlinked Datasets <a href="#en-b0014" id="en-b0014"></a>

The ecoEditor gives you access to the production database of ecoinvent; in other words, you have access to the latest accepted status of all unlinked datasets of the future version of the ecoinvent database. With the ecoEditor tool you can download these datasets (e.g. in order to prepare a corrected version of these data or to establish a new dataset on the basis of one of the existing datasets).

The download requires a data provider account, for which you can apply directly from the software, and is limited to ten datasets per day.

We hope you will enjoy working with the ecoEditor and are looking forward to your contributions for the improvement and expansion of our database.
